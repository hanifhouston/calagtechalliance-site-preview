Where We WorkNorthRedwood

Where the coast's working lands connect to statewide expertise.

The Redwood region links Del Norte, Humboldt, Lake, and Mendocino counties with innovation hubs, research centers, and online training.

On this page
OverviewPartnersProgramsOpportunities & eventsStoriesContact

Region overview

Geography, crops, and sectors.

In Redwood, partners support drone educator training and agricultural robotics learning. Chico State’s online Technical Assistance Provider Certification connects the region with professional training in regenerative agriculture and conservation planning.

ChicoSTART’s innovation hub serves Del Norte, Humboldt, Lake, and Mendocino counties, connecting founders with commercialization pathways, research opportunities, and capital access. The joint North State–Redwood Coast Ag-Tech project includes additional planned business assistance for agricultural entrepreneurs.
