Where We WorkNorthNorth State

Where rural colleges and innovation hubs bring agricultural technology to the far north.

The North State connects ten counties of ranching and specialty agriculture with ChicoSTART, Shasta College, and Chico State programs.

On this pageOverviewPartnersProgramsOpportunities & eventsStoriesContact

Region overview

Geography, crops, and sectors.

North State partners support drone education, agricultural robotics, and field-based career exploration. Chico State adds professional training in regenerative agriculture and conservation planning; AgSTEP’s announced expansion includes Shasta College.

The North State connects agricultural entrepreneurs with ChicoSTART’s commercialization pathways, research opportunities, capital connections, and individual business advising. The North State–Redwood Coast Ag-Tech project adds regional collaboration and planned small-business technical assistance.
