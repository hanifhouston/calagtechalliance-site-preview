Press ReleaseDAVIS, Calif. — November 12, 2025 — Partners announce a new season of agricultural field days.

The California AgTech Alliance shares this announcement with partners, members, and the press across California.

The announcement is part of the Workforce Development pillar, delivered through FARMS Program & Field Days. Partners across the network will share details with the growers, workers, learners, and companies they serve.

Quotations from Alliance and partner leadership, along with dates, locations, and application details, will be confirmed before this release is distributed.

About the California AgTech Alliance

The California AgTech Alliance connects agricultural innovation, workforce development, and entrepreneurship through people, programs, and partnerships across nine California regions. Led by UC ANR with regional partners, the Alliance supports growers, workers, learners, educators, startups, and investors with programs that move technology from research into practice. Learn more at calagtechalliance.org.
