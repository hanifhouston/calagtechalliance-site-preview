How regional and industry perspectives shape the Alliance.

The Alliance is guided by the regions and industries it serves. This page explains who advises the work, how priorities are set, and where the governing documents live.

Regional and industry perspectives

Regional and industry perspectives help shape the Alliance’s direction. Governance, an Industry Advisory Board, and an Innovation Roadmap are part of the Alliance’s approach to identifying common needs, informing priorities, and guiding collaboration across workforce development and agricultural innovation.

Industry Advisory Board

The Industry Advisory Board draws on agricultural and workforce experience to identify priority challenges and advise on relevant innovation.

Board membership, the detailed governance structure, and public accountability materials will be added here as they are confirmed.

Innovation Roadmap

The Innovation Roadmap gathers shared research and innovation priorities from across the regions so partners can see where effort is concentrated and where gaps remain.
