Who We AreAbout

Connecting agricultural expertise across California.

The California AgTech Alliance brings people, programs, and resources together to advance agricultural innovation and expand opportunities for workers and businesses.

What we do

Making expertise and opportunity easier to find.

California’s agricultural strengths span farms, research institutions, businesses, classrooms, and communities. The Alliance helps connect that expertise, making it easier for an entrepreneur to find a testing partner, a worker to discover training, or an educator to connect with an employer.

Our mission is to unite California’s agricultural regions through shared platforms, targeted investment, and collaboration that expand equitable access to good-paying jobs, entrepreneurial opportunity, and climate-smart innovation.

Led by

UC Agriculture and Natural Resources

Funded through

California Jobs First

What we’re working toward

Outcomes the Alliance aims to achieve.

Goal 01

Greater visibility for California AgTech

Broader recognition of California’s agricultural technology expertise and opportunities among entrepreneurs, investors, and industry leaders.

Goal 02

Stronger skills and career pathways

More students, farmworkers, and career changers gaining practical skills, industry credentials, and pathways into agricultural technology careers.

Goal 03

More innovations in the market and field

More startups progressing through testing and demonstration toward commercial readiness, customer adoption, and business growth.

Goal 04

More investment and job opportunities

More private capital supporting agricultural technology companies and their growth, creating opportunities for skilled workers.

Goal 05

Stronger connections across regions

More collaboration among farmers, educators, businesses, and community organizations, with easier access to expertise, programs, and resources.

How we work

Regional expertise. Statewide coordination.

The Alliance works through partnerships across California. Each partner brings expertise and local relationships; the Alliance connects that work across regions and common priorities.

The partners’ role

Deliver programs. Bring practical expertise.

Partners provide education and training, support entrepreneurs, conduct research, and bring knowledge of the communities and industries they serve.

The Alliance’s role

Align efforts. Strengthen connections.

The Alliance supports collaboration, connects people with expertise and resources, and raises the visibility of partners’ work across California.
