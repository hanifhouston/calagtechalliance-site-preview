Purpose & approach

The shared foundation for statewide work.

California’s agricultural technology community spans many regions, organizations, and areas of expertise. The Network is the Alliance’s statewide coordination function, bringing these efforts together around shared priorities while keeping regional needs and leadership at the center.

The Alliance directly coordinates this work: convening partners, strengthening referral pathways, developing shared tools, and supporting the exchange of knowledge. These connections help reduce fragmentation, make expertise and resources easier to find, and give partners a clearer view of work happening across California.

The Network supports the Alliance’s other pillars. Workforce development prepares people to use agricultural technologies, while entrepreneurship helps new ideas move toward practical use. The Network connects both with the relationships, expertise, and resources that help their work progress.

Connecting priorities, people, and practical support.

Shared Direction

Shared Priorities & Governance

Regional and industry perspectives help shape the Alliance’s direction. Governance, an Industry Advisory Board, and an Innovation Roadmap are part of the Alliance’s approach to identifying common needs, informing priorities, and guiding collaboration across workforce development and agricultural innovation.

About governance

Regular Exchange

Regional Collaboration

Each region brings its own agricultural needs, expertise, and relationships. Partner meetings and working groups connect these perspectives, creating space to compare progress, share learning, and identify gaps or complementary efforts. The annual convening brings members, regional leaders, innovators, and partners together to strengthen relationships and help shape the next phase of the Alliance’s work.

About the annual convening

Network Navigation

Connections & Referrals

Network navigation helps people and organizations identify relevant expertise, programs, and support. The Alliance is developing referral pathways to clarify who offers particular services, where they work, and how organizations can make useful handoffs. This includes connections with regional incubators and testing partners, alongside planned guidance around regulatory requirements.

About connections and referrals

Capital & Investment

Investment Connections

The Network brings investment perspectives into conversations about agricultural innovation. The Investor Network is in development to connect investors, regional partners, and emerging technologies around areas of shared interest. These relationships complement the Innovation Grants work described under the Entrepreneurship pillar.
