California AgTech Alliance is a statewide platform connecting agricultural innovation, workforce, and entrepreneurship across California. We align programs, investment, and infrastructure so partners can do more together.

About the Alliance

One connected ecosystem.

Three reinforcing pillars, delivered by partners across nine regions.

Pillar
