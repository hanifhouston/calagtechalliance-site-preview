About

Field demonstrations and grower discussions on practical applications of agricultural technology.

Coastal Agriculture Collaborative hosts this field day with the California AgTech Alliance as part of the Statewide Network & Collaboration pillar. Expect practical examples, time for questions, and contacts you can follow up with afterward.

Who should attend

Farmers in the Central Coast area, along with partners who support them. No prior experience with the technology is required.
