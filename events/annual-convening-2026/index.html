2026 California AgTech Alliance Annual Convening

Join Alliance members and partners for a day of farmer perspectives, project showcases, and working conversations about the future of agricultural technology in California.

Date
Tuesday, October 13, 2026
Time

About

California’s AgTech ecosystem, in one room

The Alliance unites hundreds of innovators, and thousands of workers across the state behind one goal: making California the most inclusive and climate-smart agricultural innovation ecosystem in the world.

The annual convening brings members, regional leaders, innovators, and partners together for a single day to compare what they’re learning, build relationships, and help set the direction for the Alliance’s next phase.

01

Understand what adoption requires.

Hear farmer and industry perspectives on the state of California agtech and the practical needs shaping technology adoption.

02

See what the Alliance is building.

Explore partner projects and innovations in practice, and discover how the work connects across California.

03

Shape the roadmap.

Contribute to facilitated working groups on the research and innovation priorities that will guide the Alliance’s work.

04

Explore investment and market access.

Hear perspectives on regional investment, routes to market, and opportunities for collaboration and growth.
