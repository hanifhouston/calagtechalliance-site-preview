About

A one-hour online session on sensor-based irrigation scheduling for small and mid-size operations.

North State Water Collaborative hosts this webinar with the California AgTech Alliance as part of the Workforce Development pillar. Expect practical examples, time for questions, and contacts you can follow up with afterward.

Who should attend

Farmers from any region, along with partners who support them. No prior experience with the technology is required.
