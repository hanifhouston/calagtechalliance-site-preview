Turning a farm problem into a business question.

A draft founder profile about understanding a customer before developing a solution.

By Alliance staff
Published August 1, 2026

Tags

Related program

UC ANR Build

A draft founder profile about understanding a customer before developing a solution.

The work sits within the Entrepreneurship, Commercialization & Adoption pillar through UC ANR Build, and it reached people in the Statewide region who are deciding how new tools fit their operations and careers.

Partners on the ground organized the day, answered questions, and followed up with the people who wanted to go further. That follow-through is what turns a single visit into a lasting connection.

Questions from the field

Participants asked practical questions: what the technology costs to run, who maintains it, and how to find trained people. The answers pointed back to the Alliance network, where training programs, testbeds, and regional partners share what they learn.

A verified quotation from a participant will add their perspective on turning a farm problem into a business question.Participant · Statewide

The Alliance will keep sharing what partners learn as this work continues, so that growers, workers, and educators in every region can build on it.
