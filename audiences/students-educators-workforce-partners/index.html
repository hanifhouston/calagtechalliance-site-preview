Turn agricultural challenges into learning opportunities

Connect learning with the work of California agriculture. Whether you are a student exploring careers, an educator developing hands-on lessons, or a workforce partner shaping training, the California AgTech Alliance helps you find programs and relationships that bring agricultural challenges into education.

About

Students, Educators & Workforce Partners

Students need ways to explore careers and apply what they learn. Educators need preparation, curriculum resources, and connections with agriculture. Employers and workforce organizations can contribute practical problems, workplace knowledge, and insight into skills needs. The Alliance connects these roles through partner-led programs. Students can explore projects and field experiences; educators can build teaching capacity; workforce partners can help align learning with local needs. Each program has its own audience and participation route, described on its page and the delivering partner’s website.

Ways to participate

  • Students: Discuss a suitable project or career exploration program with your advisor.
  • Educators: Ask a program lead about preparation and learning resources.
  • Workforce partners: Share skills needs or explore hosting a learning experience.

Robotics learning and competition

UC ANR Innovate and the AI Institute for Next Generation Food Systems lead these connected pathways. The Farm Robotics Academy supports educators and student project learning, especially in grades 7–12. The separate Farm Robotics Challenge is a competition for student teams across several educational divisions.

Explore learning support Explore the competition
Goal

Drone education for educators

Drones Uplifting California Communities prepares educators to build drone learning in schools and colleges. Led by UC Santa Cruz CIDER with regional partners, it combines technical instruction, practical flight training, and curriculum support. Students benefit through the learning opportunities participating educators develop.

Explore educator preparation
Goal

High school career exploration

The Center for Land-Based Learning’s FARMS program connects high school students with agricultural and environmental careers through field days, leadership activities, and professional relationships. Educators support participation, while businesses and regional organizations can contribute as field-day hosts and learning partners.

Explore field experiences
Goal

Education and employer connections

The California Agricultural Workforce Development Program brings employers, colleges, and community partners together around skills needs and career pathways. Agricultural Council of California, Western Growers, and California Farmworker Foundation co-lead the work. Ask regional contacts about education partnerships and student opportunities.
