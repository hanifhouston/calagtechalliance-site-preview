Who We ServeEntrepreneur Support Organizations

Help founders navigate their next step

Connect the support you provide with agricultural expertise and programs across California. The Alliance brings together incubators, accelerators, business advisors, research organizations, and regional partners to share learning, clarify referral pathways, and help entrepreneurs find support that fits their technology, business stage, and agricultural market.

Explore partner collaboration

About

Entrepreneur Support Organizations

Your organization brings relationships and services that entrepreneurs need. Some questions also require agricultural insight, testing expertise, or support outside your usual network. The Alliance helps connect these strengths across regions and supports conversations about gaps, shared priorities, and complementary services. You continue delivering your programs, while statewide coordination makes relevant connections easier to identify. By sharing who you serve and where your expertise fits, you help build clearer pathways for founders moving from development toward commercialization.

Ways to participate

  • Share your organization’s services, service area, and participant requirements with the Alliance.
  • Bring a recurring founder need or support gap into partner discussions.
  • Share relevant program updates and events so other partners can understand your work.

What do you want to do?

Goal

Connect founders with commercialization pathways

UC ANR Innovate leads Build for early development and Connect for market-ready technologies, working with Farmhand Ventures. Use these pathways to frame referral conversations around a founder’s stage and needs. Current eligibility, cohort details, and participation requirements belong on the provider websites.

Compare commercialization pathways
Goal

Share learning with other partners

Working groups and partner meetings create space to compare progress, discuss emerging needs, and coordinate across workforce, innovation, and commercialization. Bring your experience of supporting founders, the gaps you see, and questions that could benefit from another region’s expertise.

Explore shared work
Goal

Identify complementary support

Connections & Referrals helps individuals and organizations explore relevant partners and support. Explain the services you deliver, the entrepreneurs you serve, and the expertise you are seeking. The Alliance can use that context to help you consider potential connections across its areas of work.
