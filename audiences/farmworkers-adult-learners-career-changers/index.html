About

Farmworkers, Adult Learners & Career Changers

Your knowledge of crops, equipment, workplaces, or another industry can be a starting point for further learning. As agricultural technology changes how work gets done, you may want to develop skills for your current role or explore a different career direction. The Alliance connects you with education providers, community organizations, and workforce partners that can help you understand your options. Partners deliver training and career support; the Alliance helps you find relevant contacts. Available opportunities and participation requirements depend on your region and the provider.

Ways to participate

  • Discuss your skills and career goals with a regional workforce contact.
  • Ask a training provider about requirements and available learning opportunities.
  • Share the skills you want to develop through the Alliance’s Contact page.

Agricultural workforce pathways

The California Agricultural Workforce Development Program connects farmworkers and students with training, career guidance, and work-based learning through regional partners. Agricultural Council of California, Western Growers, and California Farmworker Foundation co-lead the program. Use its regional contacts to discuss your goals and local options.

Explore the workforce program
Goal

Regional training partners

Regional partners offer different ways to build on your experience. AgSTEP recognizes agricultural workers' existing skills through college certificates, while Hartnell College offers education in agricultural mechatronics, combining mechanical, electrical, and automated systems. Explore the workforce collection and ask each provider about participation requirements and availability.

Find regional offerings
Goal

Guidance and referrals

If you are unsure where to begin, tell the Alliance what you want to learn and where you live or work. Connections and referrals can help identify relevant organizations and contacts so you can discuss training or career exploration with the appropriate provider.
