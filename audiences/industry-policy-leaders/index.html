Bring practical needs and evidence into the conversation

Help keep agricultural innovation grounded in the needs of California’s farms, businesses, and workforce. The Alliance connects industry experience and public-sector perspectives with research, education, and commercialization partners, creating opportunities to share evidence, identify challenges, and inform collaboration across regions.

Discuss an industry or policy priority

About

Industry & Policy Leaders

Your perspective helps partners understand the conditions shaping technology adoption and workforce change. Grower organizations, agricultural businesses, industry associations, and public agencies each see different needs, constraints, and opportunities. The Alliance brings those perspectives into shared learning across California, connecting operational experience with the partners developing solutions and preparing people to use them. You can contribute knowledge about industry needs, share evidence from your work, and help identify questions for further collaboration. Program delivery remains with partners; the Alliance coordinates connections and exchanges across the network.

Ways to participate

  • Describe an agricultural, workforce, policy, or regulatory challenge and who it affects.
  • Share operational evidence and lessons that could inform partner discussions.
  • Identify expertise your organization could contribute to collaboration across regions.
