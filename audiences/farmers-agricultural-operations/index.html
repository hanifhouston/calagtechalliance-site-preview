About

Farmers & Agricultural Operations

You know the conditions a technology must work in and the skills your operation needs. Your experience can shape demonstrations, training, and conversations with innovators. The Alliance connects agricultural operations with partners working on these questions across California. Delivery partners organize programs and activities; the Alliance coordinates connections and helps regional expertise reach a wider community. Whether you are evaluating a technology or planning workforce development, begin with the problem, operating conditions, and people involved.

Ways to participate

  • Share an operational challenge technology or training could help address.
  • Ask program partners about contributing grower feedback or demonstration input.
  • Describe workforce skills needs to your regional program coordinator.

Support by goal

What do you want to do?

Goal

Bring grower insight to technology development

UC ANR Connect, led by UC ANR Innovate with Farmhand Ventures, brings market-ready technologies together with agricultural priorities, grower feedback, and field demonstrations. Ask the program team about ways to contribute operational insight and current demonstration activities relevant to your crops or needs.

Explore grower engagement
Goal

Connect workforce needs with education

The California Agricultural Workforce Development Program connects specialty crop employers, colleges, and community partners. Agricultural Council of California, Western Growers, and California Farmworker Foundation co-lead the work. Regional coordinators can discuss skills needs and relevant training pathways; activities vary by region and provider.

Explore workforce partnerships
Goal

Explore field testing and adoption

The entrepreneurship pillar connects technology development with field testing, demonstrations, and adoption needs. Use it to understand the approach and identify relevant partner programs. Testing arrangements and participation depend on the provider, technology, and agricultural setting; discuss those details with the delivery team.
