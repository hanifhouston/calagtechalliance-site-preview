Find support that fits your stage

Move your agricultural technology forward with a clearer understanding of the people who will use it. The Alliance connects you with partner programs, regional expertise, and commercialization pathways that can help you develop an early idea, prepare for demonstrations, or explore your next business challenge.

Explore commercialization pathways

About

Entrepreneurs & Startups

Your next step depends on where your technology stands. You may be testing a concept, evaluating demand, preparing a product for grower feedback, or looking beyond your region for expertise. The Alliance helps you navigate these different pathways and identify relevant program partners. UC ANR Innovate and regional organizations deliver the support; the Alliance connects their work across California. Start with the agricultural problem you want to solve, the evidence you have, and the question you need to answer next.

Ways to participate

  • Describe your technology, development stage, and agricultural customer when contacting a program.
  • Share the testing question or business barrier you need help addressing.
  • Contribute your experience at relevant partner events and demonstrations.

Support by goal

What do you want to do?

Goal

Develop an early idea

UC ANR Build, led by UC ANR Innovate with Farmhand Ventures as a collaborator, supports prototype development, market evaluation, and business strategy. Explore the program if you are assessing commercial potential. Ask the delivery team about current participation requirements and available support.

Explore Build
Goal

Prepare for grower engagement

UC ANR Connect, delivered by UC ANR Innovate with Farmhand Ventures, helps companies with market-ready technologies prepare for grower feedback and field demonstrations. Cohorts reflect agricultural priorities. Check the provider website for current focus areas, selection requirements, and application information.

Explore Connect
Goal

Explore regional expertise

Regional providers bring different strengths, from shared laboratories and working-farm testing to mentoring and business advising. Compare offerings such as AgStart, Reservoir Farms, Western Growers, and the WET Center. Eligibility, locations, and availability are set by each provider.
