Understand innovation in its agricultural context

Understand the people, programs, and agricultural needs shaping innovation across California. Whether you invest in companies or fund workforce and community initiatives, the Alliance offers a starting point for exploring the ecosystem and discussing where your interests intersect with partner work.

Discuss your investment or funding interests

About

Investors & Funders

You need to understand the problems a technology addresses, the conditions in which it will operate, and the people who could benefit. The Alliance connects agricultural, research, workforce, and regional perspectives to make that context easier to explore. Partners deliver commercialization and training programs, while the Alliance coordinates relationships and shared learning across them. For investors, this offers a view of the work supporting technology development and adoption. For funders, it helps frame conversations about workforce opportunity, regional needs, and the programs addressing them.

Ways to participate

  • Share your investment focus or funding priorities with the Alliance team.
  • Contribute your perspective on capital needs and barriers facing agricultural innovation.
  • Discuss interests in company growth, workforce development, or regional opportunity.

Understand early commercialization

Learn about the early commercialization work that helps innovators examine customer needs, market potential, prototypes, and business strategy. Led by UC ANR Innovate with Farmhand Ventures collaboration, Build provides context for understanding how agricultural innovations progress toward viable businesses.

Goal

Understand grower engagement and adoption

Explore how companies with market-ready technologies engage with California growers’ needs. UC ANR Innovate and delivery partner Farmhand Ventures support customer discovery, demonstration preparation, and field demonstrations. The program offers context on operational fit and the work involved in technology adoption.
