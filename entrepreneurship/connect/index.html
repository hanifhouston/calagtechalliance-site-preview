UC ANR Connect

A grower-informed commercialization program connecting market-ready agricultural technologies with California production needs and demonstration opportunities.

Partner-delivered
On this page

Overview

Overview

UC ANR Connect helps companies with commercially available agricultural technologies understand California growers’ needs and strengthen their approach to the market. Program cohorts are shaped around agricultural priorities and bring companies together with growers, advisors, researchers, and industry partners.

Customer discovery and preparation lead into field demonstrations, where technologies can be discussed in the context of practical farming needs and operating conditions.

Who it's for

Who it's for

Connect serves startups and established companies with market-ready agricultural technologies relevant to California agriculture. Participating companies need the capacity to engage in the program and demonstrate their technology safely. Current cohort requirements are provided on the program website.

How it works

How it works

  1. Identify agricultural priorities. Growers and advisors help define the needs that shape a cohort’s focus.
  2. Select relevant technologies. Companies apply and are selected based on their fit with the cohort and its agricultural priorities.
  3. Prepare for grower engagement. Participants work on customer discovery, market positioning, communication, and demonstration preparation.
  4. Demonstrate and connect. Field days provide opportunities to demonstrate technologies, gather feedback, and develop relationships with growers and industry partners.

What it offers

What it offers

Grower insight

Feedback on operational fit and the needs shaping technology adoption.

Commercialization preparation

Support for market positioning, grower-facing communication, and demonstrations.

Field demonstrations

Opportunities to present market-ready technologies in agricultural settings.

Industry relationships

Connections with growers, UC Cooperative Extension, researchers, commodity groups, and other partners.
