Purpose & approach

Moving agricultural ideas toward practical use.

The Alliance’s entrepreneurship pillar connects agricultural research and business development with the needs of California farms. Its focus is commercialization: helping innovations progress from early concepts and prototypes to technologies that can be tested, demonstrated, and used in agriculture.

Program partners provide commercialization support, grower feedback, and connections to research and testing. The Alliance helps coordinate these efforts, connect innovators with relevant expertise, and raise the visibility of their work across regions.

Entrepreneurship reinforces workforce development by advancing technologies that create new skills needs and business opportunities. Workforce programs prepare people to use those technologies, while the Network connects innovators with partners, testing infrastructure, and investment relationships.

UC ANR Innovate

UC ANR Build

Early-stage support helps innovators develop prototypes, evaluate markets, and shape a commercialization strategy. Workshops and project support connect agricultural research with customer needs, business development, and the work required to bring a technology into use.

For: Entrepreneurs & Startups

Explore Build
Program photo

UC ANR Innovate

UC ANR Connect

Grower-informed cohorts connect companies with market-ready agricultural technologies to agricultural expertise, customer feedback, and field demonstrations. Delivered by UC ANR Innovate with Farmhand Ventures, Connect helps companies strengthen their approach to California’s agricultural market.

For: Entrepreneurs & Startups
