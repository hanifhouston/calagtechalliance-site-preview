UC ANR Build

Support for early-stage agricultural technology development, market evaluation, and commercialization strategy.

On this pageOverviewWho it's forHow it worksWhat it offersPartnersUpcoming

Overview

Overview

UC ANR Build supports the development of agricultural innovations into viable businesses. Workshops and tailored project support help teams examine the problem a technology addresses, understand potential customers, and assess the work needed to move toward commercialization.

The program focuses on early development, including prototypes, market potential, and business strategy. Support is shaped around the needs and stage of the project.

Who it's for

Who it's for

Build is intended for entrepreneurs, early-stage companies, and research innovators still developing their technology or evaluating its commercial potential. Current participation requirements and available support are provided by the program team.

How it works

How it works

Build uses workshops and tailored support to help teams work through early commercialization questions. The following areas describe that work; the support involved depends on the project and its stage of development.

  1. Define the need. Establish the agricultural problem and identify the people or businesses experiencing it.
  2. Evaluate the market. Explore whether the problem creates a viable market opportunity and how potential customers could use a solution.
  3. Test the approach. Investigate technical assumptions and gather evidence about whether the proposed solution addresses the need.
  4. Develop the business strategy. Examine customer demand, routes to market, and the costs of bringing the solution into use.
  5. Assess capital needs. Consider the funding required and the types of capital appropriate to the business.

What it offers

What it offers

Prototype development

Support for evaluating and developing an early technology concept.

Market evaluation

Work to understand customer needs, commercial potential, and market assumptions.

Commercialization strategy

Guidance on business development, routes to market, and financing considerations.
