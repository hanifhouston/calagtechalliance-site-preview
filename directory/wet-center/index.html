WET Center

Request a referralwetcenter.org

Overview

The Water, Energy and Technology Center at Fresno State supports businesses in the Central Valley’s food, agriculture, water, and energy industries. It combines business development assistance, technical and industry expertise, research connections, and venture programs. Startups can explore Valley Ventures acceleration, food entrepreneurs can ask about Culinary Catalyst, and established businesses can discuss tailored advising. Investors and regional partners can connect through the center’s entrepreneurship network. Program eligibility, applications, membership, facilities, and costs vary by offering, so ask the team which route fits your business; participation does not guarantee funding.

What they do

  • Agtech startup accelerationRuns an accelerator for later-stage agtech startups with sales and pitch training, mentoring from founders and industry leaders, investor education, and Central Valley farm tours.
  • Water and energy technology commercializationManages a state-supported innovation cluster that gives water, energy, and agtech startups legal, business, and technical help and pilot opportunities, and convenes expert reviews that guide inventors toward commercialization.
  • Business advising and investor educationMatches companies with advisors from its network of industry experts, investors, and executives, and trains founders to pitch while teaching aspiring angel investors to evaluate early-stage companies.
  • Food and community entrepreneurshipTrains local food entrepreneurs in HACCP food safety and business planning, and hosts a state-funded hub connecting Central Valley entrepreneurs, especially from underserved communities, with partner business support.

Programs and services

  • Accelerate California (Central Valley iHub)State-funded inclusive innovation hub at the WET Center that connects Central Valley entrepreneurs, especially from underserved communities, with business support from partner SBDCs, lenders, chambers, and business centers.Program website
  • Advising ServicesCustomized business advising that matches companies with advisors from the WET Center's network of industry experts, investors, and executives, based on each company's growth goals.Program website
  • Angel AcademyCohort program with two paths: founders build and practice an investor pitch, while aspiring angel investors learn to evaluate early-stage companies; both meet at a closing live-pitch session.Program website
  • BlueTechValley Innovation ClusterCalifornia Energy Commission-supported innovation cluster managed by the WET Center, offering water, energy, and agtech startups legal, business, and technical help, pilot opportunities, and links to six CSU-based hubs.Program website
  • Culinary CatalystOne-week program for local food entrepreneurs combining HACCP food-safety certification training with workshops on business planning, pricing, financing, and working with local government.Program website
  • Technology Innovation and Evaluation (TIE) ProgramExpert committee review and guidance for inventors and entrepreneurs developing water, energy, and agriculture technologies, helping them move toward commercialization and reach funding resources through BlueTechValley.Program website
  • Valley VenturesMostly online accelerator for later-stage agtech startups, with sales and pitch training, mentoring from founders and industry leaders, investor education, and Central Valley farm tours.Program website
