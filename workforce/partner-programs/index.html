Agricultural Council of California; Western Growers Association; California Farmworker Foundation

California Agricultural Workforce Development Program

Regional partnerships connect agricultural employers, colleges, and community organizations to strengthen training, curriculum, and career pathways. The program aligns education with workforce needs and supports connections to work-based learning.

For: Workers, Learners & Farmworkers; Educators & Training Providers; Farmers & Agricultural Operators

Explore the program
Program photo

UC ANR Innovate and the AI Institute for Next Generation Food Systems

Farm Robotics Challenge

Student teams investigate agricultural problems and develop, test, and present robotic or automated solutions. The competition connects technical learning with grower needs while building experience in problem-solving, teamwork, and communication.

For: Workers, Learners & Farmworkers; Educators & Training Providers

Explore the Challenge
Program photo

UC ANR Innovate and the AI Institute for Next Generation Food Systems

Farm Robotics Academy

Educator training, curriculum support, and hands-on projects connect agricultural challenges with robotics, drones, and artificial intelligence. The Academy supports educators and youth leaders guiding student learning and participation in the Farm Robotics Challenge.

For: Workers, Learners & Farmworkers; Educators & Training Providers

Explore the Academy
Program photo

UC Santa Cruz CIDER

Drones Uplifting California Communities

A train-the-trainer program that prepares educators to introduce drone learning in their schools and colleges. Technical instruction, practical flight training, and curriculum support help instructors bring these skills into the classroom.

For: Educators & Training Providers

Explore drone education
Program photo

Center for Land-Based Learning

FARMS Program & Field Days

Field experiences and professional connections introduce high school students to agricultural and environmental careers. The program combines career exploration with practical learning and leadership development.

For: Workers, Learners & Farmworkers; Educators & Training Providers
