Drones Uplifting California Communities

Educator training that expands access to drone skills and education through schools and colleges across California.

Partner-delivered
On this page

Overview

Overview

Drones Uplifting California Communities, or DUCC, uses a train-the-trainer model to build drone education capacity. The program prepares educators to develop their own technical skills and introduce drone learning into their schools and colleges.

Online instruction, practical flight training, and continuing curriculum support connect drone knowledge with teaching. Through participating educators, DUCC aims to expand access to skills with applications in agriculture and other industries.

Who it's for

Who it's for

DUCC is open to educators, with a particular focus on high school and community college instructors developing drone education. Students and community members benefit through the learning opportunities those educators create.

How it works

How it works

Regional partners coordinate participation and training. The educator pathway combines technical preparation, practical experience, and support for teaching.

  1. Build foundational knowledge. Online learning prepares educators for the FAA remote pilot knowledge exam and introduces the knowledge needed for drone operations.
  2. Develop flight skills. Practical instruction provides hands-on experience operating drones.
  3. Bring learning into the classroom. Continued support helps educators develop drone curricula and learning activities for their students.

What it offers

What it offers

Remote pilot exam preparation

Instruction supporting preparation for the FAA Part 107 knowledge exam.

Practical training

Guided flight experience and drone operation skills.

Curriculum support

Assistance translating technical learning into educational activities.

Educator connections

Regional support and opportunities to exchange teaching practices.
