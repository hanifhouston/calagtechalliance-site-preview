Overview

Sonoma County’s Economic Development Collaborative helps businesses start, grow, and expand in the county. Its personalized support connects companies with finance, workforce, and development resources. Economic indicators, city snapshots, workforce reports, and industry information also help businesses and partners understand local conditions. Entrepreneurs and agricultural businesses can use the assistance intake to describe their needs, while planners and investors can explore county data to support a location, expansion, or development decision.

What they do

  • Business assistanceThe county's Economic Development Collaborative gives Sonoma County businesses at any stage, including agricultural businesses, personalized help with startup steps, financing options, expansion, and finding employees.
  • Economic and industry dataPublishes economic indicators, city snapshots, workforce reports, and industry information, including agriculture, for businesses, planners, and investors weighing location, expansion, or development decisions.
  • Farmland protectionSonoma County Ag + Open Space protects working farms and ranches through permanent conservation easements. A pilot also buys, protects, and resells farmland to farmers and ranchers seeking small or midsize properties.

Programs and services

  • Buy-Protect-Sell Pilot ProgramAg + Open Space pilot that buys a farm or ranch, protects it with a conservation easement, and resells it to a qualified farmer or rancher seeking their own small or midsize property.Program website
  • EDC Business ServicesFree, personalized help from the county's Economic Development Collaborative for Sonoma County businesses at any stage, covering startup steps, financing options, expansion, and finding employees.Program website
  • Land for Agriculture conservation easementsSonoma County Ag + Open Space buys conservation easements from willing owners of Sonoma County farms and ranches, or accepts them as donations, permanently protecting working agricultural land.Program website
