Overview

Plug and Play connects startups, corporations, investors, universities, and government institutions through an international innovation platform. Its work includes accelerator programs, corporate-startup pilot discussions, selective early-stage investment, and support for institutional innovation ecosystems. Industry areas include AgTech, animal health, food and beverage, and sustainability. Startups can explore program fit or Silicon Valley workspace options, while industry and regional partners can discuss collaboration. Participation, investment, and workspace access depend on the relevant program, location, and terms.

What they do

  • Startup accelerationRuns industry accelerators that pair startups with corporate partners for pilots and business development, including an agtech accelerator focused on automation, analytics, and precision farming and a food and beverage accelerator.
  • Venture investmentInvests in startups across stages, mostly at seed, through its in-house venture team, and connects portfolio companies with co-investors, mentors, and corporate pilot partners.
  • Corporate and institutional partnershipsWorks with corporations, universities, government institutions, and investors across an international network, matching companies with startups whose technologies fit their operational needs and supporting institutions' innovation initiatives.
  • Silicon Valley workspaceOffers coworking and office space for startups in Sunnyvale and Redwood City.

Programs and services

  • Plug and Play Agtech Accelerator (Topeka)Agtech accelerator run from Plug and Play's Topeka, Kansas location connecting startups in automation, analytics, field monitoring, precision farming, and supply chain with agri-food corporate partners.Program website
  • Plug and Play Investment OpportunitiesPlug and Play's in-house venture team invests in startups across stages, mostly seed, and connects portfolio companies with co-investors, mentors, and corporate pilot partners.Program website
  • Plug and Play Silicon Valley Co-WorkingFurnished private offices and shared desks for founders and growing teams at Plug and Play's Silicon Valley sites in Sunnyvale, Palo Alto, Redwood City and San Jose, with amenities that vary by site. Tenants also get access to Plug and Play events, mentorship opportunities and a community of startups, investors and corporate partners.Program website
  • Silicon Valley Food & Beverage Innovation PlatformCorporate-partnered accelerator program at Plug and Play's Sunnyvale headquarters connecting food, beverage, and farm-to-table technology startups with major food companies for pilots and business development.Program website
