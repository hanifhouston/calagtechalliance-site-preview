Overview

ChicoSTART helps entrepreneurs and businesses develop ideas, find support, and connect with the North State innovation community. Its offerings bring together business resources, mentorship connections, regional technology networks, events, and co-working space. Founders can explore innovation hub programs and the NextGen initiative, while technology businesses can connect through GrowTECH and related activities. Educators and economic development partners can approach the team about referrals and regional collaboration. Program eligibility, current sessions, workspace availability, and membership costs should be confirmed with ChicoSTART.

What they do

  • Startup incubation and advisingRuns an incubator that combines a startup curriculum with mentorship and technical advising, and offers confidential one-on-one advising and training for North State entrepreneurs starting or growing a business.
  • Rural innovation hubServes as a state-designated inclusive innovation hub for rural Northern California, offering workshops, commercialization and R&D pathways, and investor connections to founders, including agtech, clean energy, and health innovators.
  • Co-working and tech communityOperates a downtown Chico co-working space for founders, independent professionals, and small teams, and brings the North State technology community together through regional tech networks and events.
  • Youth entrepreneurshipRuns a teen entrepreneurship program that helps young people turn ideas for solving real-world problems into concrete plans.

Programs and services

  • Accelerate California Inclusive Innovation HubState-designated hub offering business advising, workshops, commercialization and R&D pathways, and investor connections for founders in rural Northern California, including agtech, clean energy, and health innovators.Program website
  • ChicoSTART Co-WorkingDowntown Chico shared workspace with day passes, memberships, and private offices for founders, independent professionals, and small teams, plus member events and a startup resource library.Program website
  • ChicoSTART NextGen IncubatorSemester-long incubator cohort for founders 18 and older, combining a startup curriculum with mentorship, technical advising, and collaborative workspace in Chico.Program website
  • Technical Assistance Program (TAP)One-on-one, confidential advising, technical assistance, and training for entrepreneurs and small business owners starting or expanding a business in the North State.Program website
  • Youth Innovators LabEntrepreneurship program for teens ages 16 to 18, run alongside the NextGen incubator, that helps young people turn ideas for solving real-world problems into concrete plans.Program website
