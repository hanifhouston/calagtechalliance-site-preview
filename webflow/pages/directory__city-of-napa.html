Overview

The City of Napa's Economic Development Division supports local business growth and development. It helps companies find business resources, suitable locations, and development partners, while providing information about the city’s economy. Entrepreneurs and agricultural businesses can start with the division to discuss a proposed activity, location needs, or relevant incentives. Investors and regional partners can use its reporting and local connections to understand business conditions. Assistance and incentives depend on the location and project.

What they do

  • Business resources and referralsThe Economic Development Division connects businesses, including agricultural businesses and entrepreneurs, with resources and partners for starting or growing in Napa.
  • Site and development supportHelps companies find suitable business locations and development partners, and provides information on developer incentives for projects within Napa.
  • Local economic informationPublishes an economic dashboard and annual reporting that describe Napa's economy and business conditions for investors and regional partners.

Programs and services

  • Business Location and Resource AssistanceHelp from the City of Napa's Economic Development Division for businesses and developers looking to start, locate or grow in the city, including support with property searches, links to local business resources and real estate brokers, and information on developer incentives. The division also connects businesses with established local companies and development partners.Program website
