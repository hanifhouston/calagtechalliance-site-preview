Overview

California State University, Chico, known as Chico State, is a public university whose College of Agriculture provides agricultural education, research and support for farmers and ranchers in northern California. Students can earn bachelor's degrees in agricultural business, agricultural science, animal science, and plant and soil science, with hands-on learning at the University Farm, and a master's degree in regenerative agriculture. The college's Center for Regenerative Agriculture and Resilient Systems gives farmers and ranchers technical assistance, including conservation and grazing plans and help applying for conservation funding, and offers professional courses. Students can connect through the College of Agriculture, and farmers and ranchers can request technical assistance from the center.

Programs and services

  • College of Agriculture Bachelor's DegreesBachelor's degrees in Agricultural Business, Agricultural Science, Animal Science, and Plant and Soil Science (which has a Regenerative Agriculture option), with hands-on learning at the 800-acre University Farm.Program website
  • CRARS Technical Assistance for Farmers and RanchersConservation and grazing plans, advice on practices such as cover crops and compost, and help applying for EQIP and Healthy Soils funding for northern California farmers and ranchers.Program website
  • North Valley Food HubOnline wholesale marketplace and distribution hub, run by Chico State's Center for Regenerative Agriculture and Resilient Systems, that helps northern California farmers sell local food to institutions and other buyers.Program website
  • Regenerative Agriculture Interdisciplinary Studies MSIn-person graduate program in regenerative agriculture. Students design an interdisciplinary course plan and do research at the University Farm and Chico State's Regenerative Agriculture Demonstration Lab.Program website
  • Technical Assistance Provider (TAP) Certification and Regenerative Agriculture Professional CoursesOnline professional courses from Chico State's Center for Regenerative Agriculture on soil health, grazing, farm planning and conservation. Take single courses, or complete four for TAP certification.Program website
