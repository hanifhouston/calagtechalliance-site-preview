Privacy Policy

What this website collects, why, and how it is handled.

Updated October 1, 2026

What we collect

Contact and newsletter forms collect the details you enter. Site analytics record page visits in aggregate without identifying individuals.

How it is used

Form submissions are routed to the Alliance team members who can answer them. Newsletter addresses are used only for Alliance updates and can be removed at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or unsubscribe from updates by contacting us.
