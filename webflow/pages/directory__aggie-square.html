Overview

Aggie Square brings university research, industry partners, students, and community activity together on the UC Davis Sacramento campus. Opened in May 2025, the innovation district provides a setting for companies to explore university collaboration, connect with research expertise, and discuss space needs. Students can combine classroom learning with community engagement through Quarter at Aggie Square, while community organizations have a dedicated partnership route. Research access, space, and educational participation follow the relevant approvals or enrollment requirements.

What they do

  • Research space and collaborationHosts companies and startups in its Sacramento research laboratories, with priority access to UC Davis research core facilities and faculty collaboration, mainly in the life sciences.
  • Experiential student learningPairs a community engagement course with a Sacramento-area internship, giving UC Davis undergraduates a quarter focused on food systems, agriculture, and sustainability.
  • Community partnershipsWorks with community organizations through a dedicated partnership route that connects community priorities with university and industry activity on the UC Davis Sacramento campus.
