Overview

Reservoir Farms connects technology development with working farms. Its incubators give startups a setting to build, test, and validate agricultural products alongside growers and industry partners. Memberships and residencies combine field access, prototype resources, grower feedback, and commercialization support. Growers, research teams, and education partners can discuss trials, co-development, validation, or workforce partnerships. The wider Reservoir platform’s technical and venture-investment activities have separate routes from the farm incubators.

What they do

  • Working-farm incubatorsRuns incubator sites in the Salinas Valley and Sonoma where agtech and robotics startups test products on professionally farmed plots, with shop and office space, advising, and trial support.
  • Grower collaborationConnects startups with growers and industry partners for field trials, product feedback, and co-development that addresses production needs.
  • Venture investmentReservoir's venture fund, separate from the farm incubators, invests from pre-seed through Series A in AI and robotics startups that take on high-value agricultural tasks before expanding to other industries.
  • Research and workforce partnershipsPartners with research teams on pilots and independent validation, and with education and regional partners on internships, training, and workforce development.
