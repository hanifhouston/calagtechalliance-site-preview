Overview

UC Davis brings agricultural and environmental science together with engineering, business, and other disciplines. Its colleges and research programs educate students and work with industry on food systems, production, and emerging technologies. Innovators can explore entrepreneurship education, research translation, and commercialization support, while employers and industry partners can discuss sponsored research, internships, employee education, or specialized facilities. Each campus unit has its own participation and partnership requirements.

What they do

  • Agricultural and professional educationOffers an undergraduate major in agricultural and environmental technology, plus workshops, certificates, and courses for professionals in agtech, viticulture, winemaking, brewing, nut and tree fruit production, and wine business.
  • Farmworker safety and technology trainingTrains farmworkers, crew leaders, and employers in English and Spanish on heat illness, wildfire smoke, and injury prevention, and is developing training for work with autonomous tractors, collaborative robots, and agrivoltaics.
  • Entrepreneurship and commercializationSupports entrepreneurs through academies, a business competition with a food and agriculture sector, customer-discovery training with agtech cohorts, and startup formation help for ventures built on university research.
  • Research and industry partnershipsWorks with industry on sponsored research, faculty partnerships, student internships, employee education, and access to specialized research facilities in agriculture, food systems, and emerging technologies.

Programs and services

  • ADAPT (Advancing the Development of Agricultural workers' Proficiency with new Technologies)Training being developed by UC Davis's Western Center for Agricultural Health and Safety to prepare farmworkers to work with autonomous tractors, collaborative robots, agrivoltaics, and other emerging agricultural technologies.Program website
  • Agricultural and Environmental Technology (B.S.)Undergraduate major in the College of Agricultural and Environmental Sciences teaching students to apply data, robotics, digital tools, and AI to food, fiber, and energy production.Program website
  • UC Davis Continuing and Professional Education Agriculture ProgramsProfessional certificates and courses for farmers, industry professionals, and career changers, including viticulture, winemaking, master brewers, nuts and tree fruit education, sensory science, and wine business.Program website
  • Western Center for Agricultural Health and Safety TrainingsInteractive English and Spanish trainings on heat illness prevention, wildfire smoke, injury and illness prevention plans, and frontline supervisor safety leadership for farmworkers, crew leaders, and agricultural employers.Program website
