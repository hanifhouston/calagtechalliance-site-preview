Overview

Salinas Valley AgTech Alliance is a regional alliance identified in the California AgTech Alliance’s public launch announcement. It connects with the statewide effort to advance agricultural technology, workforce development, and entrepreneurship in the Salinas Valley. Regional project materials also associate the alliance with Reservoir Farms, which separately operates an innovation setting for resident companies. Entrepreneurs, educators, and agricultural operators can ask the statewide Alliance to identify current projects and the relevant provider. Facility services, eligibility, costs, and participation arrangements belong to each provider and must be confirmed directly.

What they do

  • Regional agtech networkServes as a regional alliance within the California AgTech Alliance, connecting Salinas Valley agricultural businesses, technology developers, education providers, and community organizations with the statewide effort.
  • Workforce and entrepreneurshipFocuses on agricultural technology, workforce development, and entrepreneurship in the Salinas Valley. Regional project materials associate the alliance with Reservoir Farms, which separately operates an innovation site for resident companies.
