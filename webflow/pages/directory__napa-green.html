Overview

Napa Green provides sustainability certification and practical technical support for the wine industry. Its vineyard and winery pathways combine assessments, improvement planning, education, and independent verification. Winegrape growers can explore regenerative vineyard practices, carbon farm planning, and irrigation assessments, while wineries can address facility resource use and emissions. The organization also offers workshops and resources for the wider wine community. Certification involves program requirements, fees, and assessments; contact the team to identify the appropriate pathway for your operation.

What they do

  • Vineyard sustainability certificationCertifies Napa County vineyards on regenerative and climate-focused practices through third-party verification, with a custom carbon farm plan, irrigation efficiency assessment, and expert consulting.
  • Winery sustainability certificationCertifies Napa Valley wineries through an integrated assessment of energy, water, materials, and emissions, with improvement planning and standards that also cover social equity and supply chain.
  • Regenerative practice adoptionSupports winegrowers adopting practices such as cover crops, compost, and no-till through peer-learning trials on single vineyard blocks with soil-health tracking, and guides growers through documentation for regenerative practice payments.
  • Sustainability educationOffers workshops and public resources on sustainability practices for the wine industry and the wider Napa Valley community.

Programs and services

  • Napa Green Vineyard CertificationThird-party-verified regenerative and climate-focused certification for Napa County vineyards, including a custom carbon farm plan, irrigation efficiency assessment, expert consulting, and audits every three years.Program website
  • Napa Green Winery CertificationThird-party sustainability certification for Napa Valley wineries, with an integrated assessment of energy, water, materials, and emissions, improvement planning, and standards covering social equity and supply chain.Program website
  • Napa One Block ChallengePeer-learning program where Napa County winegrowers pilot four stacked regenerative practices on one vineyard block, track soil health, and join field days every few months.Program website
  • Regenerating Napa GrantPractice payments for Napa-area growers who implement regenerative practices such as cover crops, compost, biochar, hedgerows, no-till, and rotational grazing, with Napa Green guiding applications and documentation.Program website
