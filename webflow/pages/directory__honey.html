Overview

Honey is a Sacramento creative agency working across brand strategy, design, marketing, and digital services. Its focus includes food, beverage, agriculture, place, and community. The agency brings audience research and strategic planning together with visual identities, campaigns, packaging, and websites. Businesses and organizations can engage Honey to clarify their positioning, communicate with customers or communities, and develop the materials that support a product, service, or public-facing initiative.

What they do

  • Brand strategy and identityUses audience research, naming, positioning, and visual identity work to help food, beverage, agriculture, and place-based organizations define how they present themselves.
  • Packaging and product communicationDesigns packaging, labels, and supporting marketing materials for food, beverage, and agricultural products.
  • Campaigns and community engagementPlans, produces, and assesses campaigns, drawing on research and workshops, for businesses and for community and place-based initiatives.
  • Website strategy and designPlans and designs websites and supporting content that connect an organization's products or services with the audiences it wants to reach.
