Action Council and Mujeres en Acción

Request a referralactioncouncil.org

Overview

Action Council supports community change through program incubation, partnerships, and learning. Mujeres en Acción began as an Action Council pilot and helps women build leadership and economic self-sufficiency through peer support and participant-led learning. Its activities address practical concerns such as education, childcare access, employment, and emergency preparedness. Community organizations can connect with the relevant team to discuss collaboration.

What they do

  • Women's leadership and economic mobilityMujeres en Acción runs participant-led peer groups where women in Monterey and Santa Cruz counties set personal goals and build leadership around education, childcare access, employment, and emergency preparedness.
  • Community program incubationAction Council supports community change in Monterey County and surrounding communities through program incubation and partnerships. Mujeres en Acción began as one of its pilots and connects women with organizations focused on equitable employment.

Programs and services

  • Peer-to-Peer Learning Circles (Mujeres en Accion)Participant-led groups where women in Monterey and Santa Cruz counties set personal goals and build leadership, with topics such as education, training, childcare access, and pathways to better-paying jobs.Program website
