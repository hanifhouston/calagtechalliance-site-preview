California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA)

Request a referralcdfa.ca.gov

Overview

The California Department of Food and Agriculture supports agriculture through statewide programs and services. Its Specialty Crop Workforce Development Program brings agricultural employers, community colleges, and other partners together around the skills needed as farming adopts new technology. Regional partner connections help farmworkers and students find training routes, while employers and educators can discuss workforce needs and career pathways. CDFA also maintains a searchable catalogue of agricultural grant programs.

What they do

  • Grants for farmers and ranchersAwards incentive and relief grants that help farmers and ranchers adopt soil health practices, install water- and energy-saving irrigation, and recover from drought or extreme weather.
  • Specialty crop competitivenessFunds projects led by industry groups, nonprofits, colleges, and other organizations that make California fruits, vegetables, tree nuts, and nursery crops more competitive.
  • Technical assistance and grower trainingFunds one-on-one technical assistance from partner providers and UC ANR on conservation planning, project design, and grant writing, and trains growers to self-certify irrigation and nitrogen management plans.
  • Agricultural workforce developmentBrings agricultural employers, community colleges, and regional partners together around the skills farming needs as it adopts new technology, connecting farmworkers and students with training and career pathways.

Programs and services

  • California Agricultural Workforce Development ProgramAlliance programConnects agricultural employers, community colleges, and learners around technology skills, curriculum, training, and work experience.Program website
  • California Underserved and Small Producer Program (CUSP)Direct relief grants for small, mid-sized, and socially disadvantaged California farmers and ranchers hurt by drought or extreme weather, with free application help from partner organizations.Program website
  • Climate Smart Agriculture Technical AssistanceOne-on-one help for farmers and ranchers from CDFA-funded technical assistance providers and UC ANR Community Education Specialists, covering project design, conservation planning, grant writing, and project management.Program website
  • Healthy Soils ProgramCDFA incentive grants and technical assistance that help California farmers and ranchers adopt soil health practices such as cover crops, compost, hedgerows, and reduced or no tillage.Program website
  • Irrigation and Nitrogen Management Training for Grower Self-CertificationA self-paced online course and exam from CDFA's Fertilizer Research and Education Program that qualifies growers to self-certify irrigation and nitrogen management plans for their own operations.Program website
  • Specialty Crop Block Grant ProgramCDFA's annual competitive grants for projects that make California fruits, vegetables, tree nuts, and nursery crops more competitive. Industry groups, businesses, nonprofits, tribes, agencies, and colleges can apply; individual farmers cannot.Program website
  • State Water Efficiency and Enhancement Program (SWEEP)CDFA grants that help farmers and ranchers install water- and energy-saving irrigation upgrades, such as soil moisture monitoring, drip systems, pump improvements, and renewable energy for pumping.Program website
