Overview

The City of Davis provides municipal services and business information for companies operating in or considering Davis. Its economic development work connects local businesses with city processes, university partners, and regional organizations. Agriculture and food technology are among the industries identified in the city’s economic development strategy. Entrepreneurs and investors can use the city as an entry point for establishment, development, municipal requirements, and questions about the local innovation economy.

What they do

  • Business establishment informationProvides information on business licenses, utilities, local resources, and municipal requirements for companies operating in or considering Davis.
  • Economic development servicesThe city's economic development office routes business, investment, and development questions to the appropriate city staff and connects local companies with UC Davis and regional organizations.
  • Innovation economy strategyIts economic development strategy names agriculture and food technology among the innovation industries it targets and sets priorities for business support, workforce, and partnerships.
