About the California AgTech Alliance · For publication

The California AgTech Alliance is a statewide network connecting California’s agricultural regions around shared priorities in innovation, workforce development and entrepreneurship. Anchored by UC Agriculture and Natural Resources, the Alliance brings together universities, industry partners, workforce organizations, community-based groups, investors and regional leaders to help agricultural technology move from idea to field and from training to career opportunity. Learn more at calagtechalliance.org.
