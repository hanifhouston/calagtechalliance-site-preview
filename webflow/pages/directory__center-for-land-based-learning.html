Overview

The Center for Land-Based Learning helps people build careers and connections in agriculture and natural resource stewardship. Its programs combine practical agricultural learning, job experience, habitat restoration, and community food access. Youth, aspiring farmers, agricultural employees, educators, and employers can explore pathways that connect classroom learning with work in the field. The Farm Manager Apprenticeship brings paid employment, technical instruction, and leadership development together through participating employers.

What they do

  • Farmer training and incubationTrains aspiring, beginning, and experienced farmers through hands-on courses on equipment, irrigation, and farm business, and provides beginning farmers with leased land, shared infrastructure, and mentoring in Yolo County.
  • Agricultural career advancementPrepares agricultural employees for supervisor and manager roles through paid apprenticeships with farm employers, and offers employer-supported leadership training for Spanish-speaking workers in communication, workplace English, digital skills, and safety.
  • Youth agriculture and conservationEngages high school students in habitat restoration on farms and ranches and in paid urban agriculture internships in Sacramento that combine school garden work with career learning.
  • Community food accessTrains Yolo County residents in local food systems and healthy eating, and operates a mobile farmers market that supports local agriculture and food access.

Programs and services

  • AgHiREHybrid leadership program for Spanish-speaking agricultural employees, supported by their employers. It covers leadership and communication, workplace English, digital skills, and workplace safety and labor rules.Program website
  • California Farm Academy CoursesShort hands-on courses and workshops for aspiring, beginning and experienced farmers on topics such as welding, tractor operation, irrigation, animal husbandry, farm business planning and marketing.Program website
  • Community Food AmbassadorsCourse with a stipend on local food systems and healthy eating for Yolo County residents. Graduates can go on to work at CLBL's Mobile Farmers Market and promote local agriculture.Program website
  • Farm Business IncubatorLand leases with shared infrastructure and ongoing mentoring in Yolo County for beginning farmers starting their own farm businesses, testing production methods and building markets.Program website
  • Farm Manager ApprenticeshipState-registered apprenticeship that combines paid on-the-job training with participating farm employers, agricultural coursework and leadership development. It prepares agricultural workers for supervisor and manager roles.Program website
  • SLEWS ProgramHigh school students restore habitat on a farm or ranch over several field days, planting native vegetation and learning about conservation careers alongside mentors and landowners.Program website
  • Urban Ag Internship ProgramYear-long paid internship for Sacramento high school students. It combines school garden work, workforce development classes, farm field trips and guest speakers on agriculture and environmental careers.Program website
