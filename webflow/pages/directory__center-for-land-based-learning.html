Overview

The Center for Land-Based Learning (CLBL) connects people to opportunities in agriculture, environmental stewardship and local food systems through hands-on education and real-world experiences. Our programs are organized around three pillars: Workforce Development, Land & Environment, and Community Food & Farms.

Through these programs, CLBL helps young people explore careers, supports agricultural employees and entrepreneurs, restores habitat and strengthens access to fresh, locally grown food. California Farm Academy offers flexible courses and workshops for people interested in learning more about agriculture, including current farmers and aspiring entrepreneurs.

What they do

  • Farmer training and incubationCalifornia Farm Academy offers flexible, hands-on courses and workshops through its Explorer Pathway. Opportunities are open to anyone interested in agriculture, including current farmers and aspiring entrepreneurs. CLBL has also supported beginning farmers through its Farm Business Incubator, which is currently paused.
  • Agricultural career advancementAgHiRE helps experienced, primarily Spanish-speaking agricultural employees strengthen leadership, communication and workplace skills, with employers investing alongside them. The Farm Manager Apprenticeship combines paid, on-the-job training with classroom instruction to prepare the next generation of agricultural managers.
  • Youth agriculture and conservationFARMS connects high school students with farmers, scientists and agricultural professionals through multiple field days over the school year. SLEWS brings students together with landowners and conservation professionals for hands-on habitat restoration and environmental stewardship.
  • Community food accessCLBL's Community Food Program brings fresh, locally grown food into communities through the Mobile Farmers Market. We purchase produce from small and beginning farmers, giving growers another market while expanding access to fresh food. Community Food Ambassadors also support nutrition education and community outreach.

Programs and services

  • FARMS ProgramAlliance programFARMS connects high school students with agriculture and environmental careers through hands-on field days throughout the school year. Students learn alongside farmers, scientists and industry professionals, gaining real-world experience and exploring future career opportunities.Program website
  • AgHiRE ProgramAgHiRE helps experienced, primarily Spanish-speaking agricultural employees strengthen leadership, communication, English, digital literacy and workplace skills. The 12-week program prepares participants to grow into supervisory roles, with employers investing alongside them.Program website
  • California Farm Academy CoursesCalifornia Farm Academy's Explorer Pathway offers flexible, hands-on courses and workshops for anyone interested in agriculture, including current farmers, curious learners and aspiring entrepreneurs. Topics include irrigation, tractor training, welding, crop and farm business planning, and more.Program website
  • Community Food AmbassadorsCommunity Food Ambassadors build knowledge of local food systems, nutrition and community resources, then share what they've learned through outreach, education and support at Mobile Farmers Market stops.Program website
  • Farm & Climate ProgramCLBL's Farm & Climate program connects agricultural practices with environmental stewardship through hands-on learning, demonstration projects and climate-smart approaches to land management.Program website
  • Farm Business IncubatorCLBL's Farm Business Incubator has supported beginning farmers with access to land, shared infrastructure and mentorship as they establish agricultural businesses. The program is currently paused and not accepting applications.Program website
  • Farm Manager ApprenticeshipThe state-registered Farm Manager Apprenticeship combines paid, on-the-job training with agricultural coursework and leadership development. Apprentices gain practical experience alongside participating employers while preparing for farm management and supervisory roles. *Currently on hold and not accepting applications.Program website
  • Mobile Farmers MarketCLBL's Mobile Farmers Market brings fresh, locally grown produce directly into communities across the Sacramento region. By purchasing from small and beginning farmers, the program creates markets for local growers while expanding access to healthy food.Program website
  • SLEWS ProgramSLEWS brings high school students together with landowners and conservation professionals for hands-on habitat restoration. Through multiple field days throughout the school year, students plant, monitor and restore native habitat while developing environmental stewardship skills.Program website
  • Urban Ag Internship ProgramYear-long paid internship for Sacramento high school students. It combines school garden work, workforce development classes, farm field trips and guest speakers on agriculture and environmental careers.Program website
