Overview

REAP Climate Center connects ecological learning, local climate action, and pathways toward nature-based employment. Its Alameda campus supports urban farming, habitat restoration, regenerative exhibits, makerspace activities, and partner organizations. Community members can explore volunteering and practical ecological projects, while educators and organizations can discuss learning experiences or collaboration. Partner-developed training is part of its workforce approach, although some program areas are still developing. Check the current catalogue and contact REAP before planning a course or visit.

What they do

  • Nature-based skills trainingIts workforce approach includes partner-developed, hands-on certificate courses in regenerative landscaping, composting, biochar, vermiculture, fungi-based bioremediation, and habitat restoration, plus vocational training in regenerative food service and logistics.
  • Ecological learning and volunteeringThe Alameda campus hosts urban farming, habitat restoration, regenerative exhibits, and makerspace activities where Bay Area community members, students, and volunteers take part in practical ecological projects.
  • Campus partnershipsShares its campus with partner organizations and works with educators, companies, and community groups on climate learning and nature-based solutions.
