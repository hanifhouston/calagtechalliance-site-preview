Overview

Community Alliance with Family Farmers, known as CAFF, supports resilient family farms, communities, and ecosystems through practical services and policy advocacy. Its programs address ecological farming, farm business needs, food safety, market access, and appropriate technology. Small farms and local food systems are central to its work. California growers can explore farm resources and the Small Farm Tech Hub, while community and policy partners can connect with farmer perspectives and networks.

What they do

  • Ecological and organic farmingProvides on-farm technical assistance, field days, and research on ecological pest management, hedgerows, cover crops, compost, and soil health, and helps farmers transition to or maintain organic certification.
  • Market access and food safetyHelps family farms sell to schools, hospitals, universities, and other buyers, and helps small and underserved farms meet food safety rules through workshops and on-farm assistance in English, Spanish, and Hmong.
  • Appropriate technology for small farmsSupports small farms, farmers markets, and food hubs in adopting e-commerce, point-of-sale, and digital marketing tools, and co-hosts a competition for practical hardware, software, and DIY solutions for small farms.
  • Farmer-centered policy advocacyAdvocates for policies that support small family farms, local food systems, and resilient communities, bringing farmer perspectives to policy partners through regional chapters across California.

Programs and services

  • Ecological Farming Technical AssistanceOn-farm technical assistance, field days, webinars, and research on ecological pest management, hedgerows, cover crops, compost, and soil health, including Healthy Soils Program application support.Program website
  • Farm to Market (Farm to Cafeteria and marketing support)Technical assistance, marketing classes, and farmer-buyer networking mixers that help family farms sell to schools, hospitals, universities, CSA members, and other buyers.Program website
  • Food Safety ServicesWorkshops, webinars, one-on-one farm visits, plan templates, and audit preparation that help small and underserved farms meet food safety rules and Good Agricultural Practices, in English, Spanish, and Hmong.Program website
  • Organic Certification SupportOne-on-one technical assistance, mentor matching with experienced organic growers, field days, and a certifier comparison tool for California farmers transitioning to or renewing organic certification.Program website
  • Small Farm Tech HubOne-on-one consultations, guides, workshops, and peer communities that help small farms, farmers markets, and food hubs adopt e-commerce, point-of-sale, digital marketing, and other appropriate technology.Program website
