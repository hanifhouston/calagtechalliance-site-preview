Overview

The F3 (Farms Food Future) Initiative is a coalition working to make California's Central Valley a competitive, sustainable and inclusive agtech hub. The Central Valley Community Foundation is its backbone organization, supporting collaboration, strategic planning, fundraising and evaluation. The initiative serves Fresno, Kings, Madera, Merced and Tulare counties. Its work runs through three components: F3 Innovate for agtech research and commercialization, F3 Local for small farms and food entrepreneurs, and AgTEC Workforce for agricultural skills training. F3 Innovate's commercialization and student programs are run by F3i, a nonprofit, and F3 Local is an initiative of UC Agriculture and Natural Resources with community partners. Merced College leads AgTEC Workforce, which offers a competency-based Ag Systems Certificate through Central Valley community colleges.

What they do

  • Agtech research and commercializationSupports the development, field testing and adoption of new agricultural technology in the Central Valley. Offers grants, industry connections and shared computing to startups, researchers and growers.
  • Small farm and food business supportHelps small-scale farmers try appropriate technology through shared equipment, on-farm trials and one-on-one guidance. Gives food entrepreneurs business advising, training, marketing help and connections to local buyers.
  • Agricultural workforce trainingOffers a free, self-paced, competency-based certificate in agricultural systems through Central Valley community colleges, in English and Spanish. Its curriculum was informed by farmworker surveys and industry input and prepares learners for agricultural technology jobs.
  • Student agtech pathwaysIntroduces students to agricultural technology through paid internships and applied AI and data learning.

Programs and services

  • AgTEC Ag Systems CertificateA free, competency-based agricultural systems certificate offered through Central Valley community colleges in English and Spanish, preparing learners for agricultural technology jobs.Program website
  • AI LaunchpadAn applied AI, data science and cloud computing program for high school and community college students, built on Central Valley weather, soil and crop data.Program website
  • Central to the Valley Brand CampaignA regional local-food brand, funded by F3 Local with the Community Alliance with Family Farmers, that gives farms and food businesses onboarding toolkits, a listing in a buyer map and a partner network.Program website
  • Commercialization EngineConnects growers and agtech companies through industry challenge workshops, an R&D library, market-entry preparation and technology transfer support, helping field-ready solutions move toward adoption on farms.Program website
  • Equipment Lending LibraryShort-term loans of specialized, scale-appropriate farm tools and equipment, with required training, so small Central Valley farms can try technology before buying.Program website
  • F3i Supercomputing CenterRegional AI and data-processing infrastructure for agricultural modeling and research, giving agtech startups and researchers access to computing capacity and shared agricultural data.Program website
  • Farm InnovationConnects Central Valley growers with UC researchers and product developers to run field trials, share farmer feedback with developers, and offer hands-on training on irrigation, soil health and crop management.Program website
  • Food Entrepreneurship Academy (Business Launchpad)Training and coaching on business planning, licensing, food safety, and permitting for aspiring food entrepreneurs, especially mobile food vendors and cottage food operators, with connections to shared kitchens.Program website
  • Innovation GrantsCompetitive grants with mentorship, pilot coordination and commercialization support for teams ready to field-test and validate agricultural technologies with growers and operators in the Central Valley.Program website
  • Small Farm Food Aggregation HubsAggregation hubs that combine products from small farms and food businesses for coordinated distribution to schools, hospitals, and grocers, helping producers reach institutional and wholesale buyers.Program website
  • TerraTech Summer InternshipA paid summer internship placing undergraduate and graduate students with Central Valley companies, startups, nonprofits and public agencies on food and agriculture projects in AI, data science, automation, robotics and sustainability.Program website
  • Wrap Around Business ServicesOne-on-one consulting for food entrepreneurs on licensing and permits, product development, food safety compliance, marketing and funding navigation.Program website
