Accessibility

How this website is built to be used by everyone, and how to tell us when it is not.

Updated October 1, 2026

Our commitment

The Alliance aims to meet WCAG 2.1 AA across this site: readable contrast, keyboard access, captions, and plain language.

Pages are checked when they are published and again when the design system changes.

Known limitations

Some partner-supplied documents and embedded forms may not yet meet these standards. We flag them where we can and ask partners for accessible versions.

Tell us

If something on this site is hard to use, send a note through the contact form with Accessibility as the subject and we will respond within ten working days.
