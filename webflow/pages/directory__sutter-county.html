Overview

Sutter County connects agricultural businesses and residents with county services and business-support resources. Its economic development portal brings together startup information, planning resources, local economic information, and referrals to external organizations. The Agricultural Department administers local agricultural and weights-and-measures programs, publishing crop reports and information on pest management, pesticides, inspections, and fees. Businesses, educators, and regional partners can use these public resources to understand local requirements and conditions. The appropriate route depends on the service and county jurisdiction.

What they do

  • Business resourcesThe county's economic development portal gathers startup information, planning resources, property records, and referrals to outside business-support organizations for Sutter County businesses.
  • Agricultural programs and inspectionsThe Agricultural Department administers local agricultural and weights-and-measures programs, with information on pest management, pesticide use, inspections, and local agricultural requirements.
  • Crop and economic informationPublishes crop reports and local economic information that businesses, educators, and regional partners use for research and planning.
