Overview

Napa Valley Vintners works to promote, protect, and enhance the Napa Valley wine region. It supports wineries through marketing, advocacy, member resources, and professional programs, alongside environmental leadership and community stewardship. Napa Valley wineries can explore membership-based services, while wine trade and community partners can connect with educational resources and professional programs. Its marketing and industry outreach reaches wine trade, consumers, and media internationally.

What they do

  • Winery marketing and advocacyPromotes and protects the Napa Valley wine region on behalf of member wineries through marketing, industry advocacy, and member resources, reaching wine trade, consumers, and media internationally.
  • Trade and professional developmentProvides trade resources that connect buyers and industry professionals with Napa wines, and runs leadership development for Napa Valley wine industry professionals.
  • Environmental and community stewardshipSupports environmental stewardship and community investment in Napa Valley and provides educational resources about the wine region.

Programs and services

  • NVV Leadership ProgramA nine-month cohort program for Napa Valley wine industry professionals, combining in-person sessions, speakers, readings, and a group project addressing an industry or community need.Program website
