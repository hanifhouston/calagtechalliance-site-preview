Overview

AgTech Innovation Alliance is the nonprofit organization behind AgStart, an incubation program for agricultural, food, and health technology entrepreneurs in Woodland. Through AgStart, founders can explore shared innovation facilities, mentoring, education, and connections to the regional startup community. The program brings laboratory and workspace options together with support for developing science-based businesses. Entrepreneurs should contact AgStart about program fit, facility requirements, pricing, and current participation opportunities; potential mentors and partners can approach the same team about contributing expertise or support.

What they do

  • Startup incubationOperates AgStart, a Woodland incubation program for agriculture, food, and health technology entrepreneurs developing science-based businesses in the Greater Sacramento region.
  • Shared labs and workspaceProvides shared wet-lab, fermentation, tissue-culture, and food lab facilities alongside co-working, offices, and meeting space for startups doing bench-level commercialization work.
  • Founder mentoring and educationPairs founders with volunteer mentors for one-on-one business and technology advice, and offers founder education and networking that connect entrepreneurs, mentors, and partners in the regional startup community.

Programs and services

  • AgStart Office HoursOne-on-one sessions that pair agriculture and food entrepreneurs with volunteer mentors to talk through business and technology needs, offered in person or remotely.Program website
  • Raley's Food LabA certified commercial kitchen at AgStart, reserved by the hour, where food entrepreneurs and product developers create ingredients and recipes and prototype new food products.Program website
  • The Lab@AgStartShared wet-lab benches, fermentation and tissue-culture labs, co-working, offices, and meeting space in Woodland for agriculture, food, and health startups doing bench-level commercialization.Program website
