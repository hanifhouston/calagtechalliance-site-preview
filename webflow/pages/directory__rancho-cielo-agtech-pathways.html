Overview

Rancho Cielo Youth Campus is a Monterey County nonprofit that helps young people facing challenges earn a high school diploma and prepare for work through vocational training. On its Salinas campus, students earn diplomas through John Muir Charter Schools, receive counseling and life-skills support, and train in trades including agricultural technology, auto and diesel repair, construction, welding and culinary arts. Its Ag Technology & Mechatronics program gives young people ages 16 to 24 hands-on agriculture projects and credentials such as the FAA Part 107 drone certificate and OSHA safety training. Young people can apply online or on paper, and businesses and community members can support students as sponsors, partners or mentors.

What they do

  • Agriculture and technical trainingCombines academics with hands-on study in animal science, agribusiness, plant science, agricultural mechanics, irrigation and farm technology, and drones for Monterey County young people without a high school diploma.
  • Certifications and career pathwaysPrepares students for industry certifications in drones, agriculture safety, and animal care, and connects them with agricultural employers and pathways into college agriculture and mechatronics study.
  • Student support servicesProvides transportation, essential resources, and wellness support alongside academic and vocational instruction.
