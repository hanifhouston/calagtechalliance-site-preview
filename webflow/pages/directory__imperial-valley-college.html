Overview

Imperial Valley College prepares students for agricultural careers and further study. Its Agriculture Plant Science pathway combines biological and physical science with agricultural applications, offering certificate and transfer-degree options. Students can explore careers ranging from crop consulting and farm management to land-use planning. The college's Learning to Lead program adds academic, internship, leadership, and transfer support for eligible agriculture majors. Educators and employers can discuss partnerships that connect classroom learning with hands-on experience.

What they do

  • Plant science and agribusiness educationOffers transfer degrees and certificates in agriculture plant science and agriculture business, preparing Imperial Valley students for CSU transfer or careers in crop consulting, farm management, inspection, processing, and marketing.
  • Agricultural equipment technologyOffers degrees and certificates in maintaining and repairing farm tractors, diesel and heavy equipment, hydraulics, and automation systems, including preparation for ASE diesel exams and farm equipment technician jobs.
  • Internships and transfer supportOffers agriculture majors paid internships with agencies and local agricultural employers, along with leadership development, counseling, and scholarships for transfer to partner universities, through a USDA-funded program.

Programs and services

  • Agriculture Business (A.S.-T and Agriculture Business Management Certificate)Transfer degree and certificate applying business and management concepts to agriculture, for students heading toward careers in farm operations, farm services, processing, marketing, and distribution of farm products.Program website
  • Agriculture Plant Science (A.S.-T and Certificate)Associate degree for transfer and certificate applying biological and physical sciences to agriculture, preparing Imperial Valley students for CSU transfer or careers such as crop consultant, inspector, or farm manager.Program website
  • Agriculture Service Technology (A.S. and Certificate)Associate degree and certificate preparing students to maintain and repair agricultural and heavy equipment and automation systems, covering diesel engines, hydraulics and pneumatics, welding, industrial automation, and workplace safety.Program website
  • Diesel Farm Machinery and Heavy Equipment Technician (Certificate)Career technical certificate in maintaining and repairing farm tractors, agricultural hydraulic equipment, and other diesel and heavy machinery, preparing students for ASE diesel exams and jobs such as farm equipment technician.Program website
  • Learning to Lead (L2L) ProgramUSDA-funded program for IVC agriculture majors offering paid internships with agencies and local ag employers, leadership conferences, counseling and workshops, and scholarships for transfer to partner universities.Program website
