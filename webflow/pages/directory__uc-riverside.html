Overview

UC Riverside’s Office of Technology Partnerships connects agricultural innovators with university-developed technologies and research relationships. Its available technology portfolio spans agriculture, life sciences, engineering, sustainability, and software. Founders and prospective partners can explore inventions, discuss licensing or further development, and connect through corporate partnerships or entrepreneurs-in-residence. A suitable route depends on the technical question, the individual technology, and its stage of readiness.

What they do

  • University technology licensingOffers more than 500 UC Riverside inventions for licensing or further development through research partnerships, including patented avocado, citrus, and asparagus varieties.
  • Customer discovery and grant readinessTrains researchers, students, and regional innovators, including in sustainable farming, to test whether their technologies meet real market needs, and prepares teams for federal SBIR and STTR proposals.
  • Startup incubation and growthRuns a downtown Riverside incubator with coworking space, mentoring, workshops, and funding-readiness support for early-stage startups, plus growth coaching for established product companies and mentor office hours.
  • Clean citrus stockMaintains disease-tested citrus varieties, supplies clean budwood to nurseries and growers, and manages the introduction of new citrus varieties into California.

Programs and services

  • BizTech ConnectFour-week virtual growth program with coaching on strategy, customer acquisition, operations, and financial planning for established, revenue-generating product companies in Inland Southern California.Program website
  • Citrus Clonal Protection ProgramDisease-tested citrus varieties and clean budwood for California nurseries and growers.Program website
  • ExCITE Riverside IncubatorDowntown Riverside incubator offering flexible coworking space, mentoring through UCR's EPIC Small Business Development Center, workshops, networking events, and funding-readiness support for early-stage startups, including agtech companies.Program website
  • INNOVAR Entrepreneurial TrainingSeven-week Lean LaunchPad customer-discovery program in the NSF I-Corps Hub West that helps researchers, students, and regional innovators, including in sustainable farming, test whether their technology meets a real market need.Program website
  • NSF I-Corps ZAP Program (Hub West)Short virtual customer-discovery program for science and engineering innovators, offered through the NSF I-Corps Hub West, with a path toward the national I-Corps Teams program.Program website
  • SBIR Phase I WorkshopSix-week team workshop combining customer discovery, entrepreneur-in-residence mentoring, and commercialization planning for companies and innovators preparing federal SBIR or STTR Phase I proposals.Program website
  • UCR Available Technologies (Technology Licensing)Searchable portfolio of more than 500 UC Riverside inventions, including patented plant varieties such as avocado, citrus, and asparagus, that companies and founders can license or develop through research partnerships.Program website
