Overview

Reedley College provides college and career education in California’s agricultural Central Valley. Its published catalog describes agriculture business, plant and animal science, agricultural mechanics, forestry, and related technical pathways, with certificate, associate degree, and university transfer options. Students can ask counseling about the program that fits their goal, while agricultural employers can discuss workforce needs and available training. High school students and educators can explore dual enrollment and enrichment routes. The reviewed catalog is from 2023–24, so current credentials, requirements, and course schedules should be confirmed with the college.

What they do

  • Agriculture and natural resources degreesOffers degrees, certificates, and transfer pathways in agriculture business, animal science, agricultural education, enology, environmental horticulture, food safety, forestry, and natural resources, with dedicated pathway counseling.
  • Agricultural technologyPrepares students for technology-driven jobs in the local fresh fruit industry, from field production and data management to automated processing, and is planning a center pairing students with agtech startups for internships.
  • Equipment and crop technician trainingTrains equipment service technicians in dealer-style labs, and prepares irrigation, fertility, and pest management technicians and future pest control advisors using the 300-acre school farm and greenhouses.
  • Noncredit training for working adultsOffers noncredit courses for adults and current workers in agricultural technology, food safety, forklift operation, ag mechanics, and welding, including a competency-based certificate with bilingual support for farmworkers.

Programs and services

  • Ag Technology ProgramAssociate degree and certificates preparing students for technology-driven agriculture jobs, from field production and data management to automated processing and food safety, focused on the local fresh fruit industry.Program website
  • AgSTEP Agriculture Systems CertificateA noncredit, competency-based community college certificate in which farmworkers and other agricultural learners build on skills they already have and gain agtech skills, with bilingual support and stackable pathways toward degrees.Program website
  • AgTEC Innovation CenterPlanned center at Reedley College that will host agricultural technology startups and give regional students internships, job shadows, and part-time work with them.Program website
  • Extended Learning Agriculture Technology (Noncredit)Noncredit courses for adults and current workers, including an Ag Technology certificate on the fresh fruit industry, emerging agricultural technologies, and HACCP food safety, plus forklift, ag mechanics, and welding courses.Program website
  • Mechanized Agriculture (Equipment Service Technician) ProgramAssociate degree and certificates that train equipment service technicians on engines, transmissions, hydraulics, and electrical systems in dealer-style labs, supported by Quinn Company, the regional Caterpillar dealer.Program website
  • Plant and Soil Science ProgramTransfer and associate degrees plus certificates for irrigation, fertility, and pest management technicians, plant protection interns, and pest control advisor preparation, using the college's 300-acre school farm and greenhouses.Program website
