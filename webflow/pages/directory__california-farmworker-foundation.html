Overview

California Farmworker Foundation, known as CFF, provides programs and services that improve farmworkers' quality of life and help them become leaders and advocates for their communities. The nonprofit serves farmworkers in Fresno, Madera, Tulare, Kern, Santa Barbara and Riverside counties, with work spanning education, workforce development, health and wellness, and civic assistance. Its services include scholarships for farmworkers' children, health education, help with public benefits, and immigration clinics. CFF also helps deliver the California Department of Food and Agriculture's Specialty Crop Workforce Development Program, which helps agricultural workers build skills as farming adopts new technology. Farmworkers and their families can connect through its offices in Delano, Fresno, Huron, Santa Maria and Coachella.
