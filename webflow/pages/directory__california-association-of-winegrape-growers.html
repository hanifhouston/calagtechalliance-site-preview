Overview

The California Association of Winegrape Growers (CAWG) represents winegrape growers through state and federal advocacy, industry information, education, and member programs. It helps vineyard businesses follow issues affecting their operations and engages policymakers and regulators on grower priorities. Researchers and technology developers can use its public resources to learn about vineyard concerns, while grower and associate memberships provide additional ways to connect with the winegrape community. Benefits depend on membership eligibility and program terms.

What they do

  • State and federal advocacyRepresents California winegrape growers on state and federal policy, engaging policymakers and regulators in Sacramento and Washington, D.C., on issues affecting vineyard operations.
  • Grower information and educationKeeps vineyard businesses informed through policy updates, webinars, and education, and publishes resources on vineyard mapping, sustainability, wildfire and smoke exposure, and autonomous equipment.
  • Membership and industry connectionsOffers grower and associate memberships, with member programs on topics such as insurance and compliance, that connect vineyard operators and allied businesses with the wider winegrape community.
  • Student scholarshipsSupports college scholarships for students whose parents or guardians work for winegrape growers and for Central Coast students studying viticulture or enology.

Programs and services

  • California Wine Grape Growers Foundation ScholarshipsAnnual college scholarships for California high school seniors whose parent or guardian works for a winegrape grower, plus a memorial scholarship for Central Coast students studying viticulture or enology.Program website
