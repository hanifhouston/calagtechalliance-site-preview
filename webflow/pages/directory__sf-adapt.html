Overview

SF-ADAPT connects farmers, researchers, nonprofits, and technology developers to create practical tools suited to smaller agricultural operations. Its farmer-led approach lets the people using a technology help shape its design and test whether it meets their needs. Presented through F3 Local, the collaborative network connects technology development with on-farm trials, learning workshops, and demonstrations. Participation depends on the activity and available support.

What they do

  • Farmer-led technology developmentBrings small and medium-scale farmers together with technology developers and researchers to co-create practical tools and test them through on-farm trials, pilot projects, and demonstrations at UC ANR's Kearney research center.
  • Shared farm equipmentLends specialized, scale-appropriate farm tools and equipment for short-term use, with training, so small Central Valley farms can try technology before buying it.
  • Small farm innovation and learningRuns a competition with cash prizes for practical hardware, software, DIY, and social solutions for small farms, and supports farmer learning through conference stipends, workshops, and peer networks.

Programs and services

  • SF-ADAPT Conference Stipends for Small FarmersStipends and support that help small farmers attend major agricultural and technology conferences, where they can learn about new tools and connect with peers, researchers, and developers.Program website
  • SF-ADAPT Innovation ConnectionConnects small and medium-scale farmers with technology developers and researchers to co-create tools and run on-farm trials and pilot projects, with testing and equipment demonstrations at Kearney REC.Program website
