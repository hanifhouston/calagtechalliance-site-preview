Overview

Fresno County Economic Development Corporation, or Fresno EDC, helps companies locate, grow, and operate in Fresno County. It works with business, government, and civic partners on investment attraction, business retention, workforce needs, and regional competitiveness. Agricultural and technology businesses can discuss location or expansion projects, identify relevant local resources, and explore connections to hiring and training support. Economic research and regional information help businesses and partners plan their next steps.

What they do

  • Business location and expansionAssists companies locating, expanding, or staying in Fresno County with site selection, incentive and permitting guidance, utility cost analysis, and financing referrals.
  • Employer hiring supportConnects Fresno County employers with customized training, hiring incentives, wage subsidies, and screened job-ready candidates through county employment programs.
  • Job seeker trainingRuns an employer-led initiative offering industry-designed training, pre-apprenticeship, internships, and support services that prepare Central Valley job seekers for careers in manufacturing, construction, logistics, and business services.
  • Economic and labor market researchProvides Fresno County labor market data and city economic profiles, and prepares custom research on workforce availability, wages, major employers, and industries for businesses, investors, and site selectors.
