Find your place in the Alliance

California’s agricultural strengths span farms, research institutions, businesses, classrooms, and communities. Whether you’re an entrepreneur looking for testing partners, a worker exploring training, or an educator connecting with employers, the Alliance connects you to the programs, partners, and opportunities that fit your work.

Entrepreneurs & Startups

Move your agricultural technology forward with a clearer understanding of the people who will use it. The Alliance connects you with partner programs, regional expertise, and commercialization pathways that can help you develop an early idea, prepare for demonstrations, or explore your next business challenge.

See all 85 programs for Entrepreneurs & Startups

Investors & Funders

Understand the people, programs, and agricultural needs shaping innovation across California. Whether you invest in companies, fund community initiatives, or lead economic development at home or abroad, the Alliance offers a starting point for exploring the ecosystem and finding where your interests intersect with partner work.

See all 6 partners for Investors & Funders

Workers & Learners

Build on your experience, explore new skills, and find a path into California’s changing agricultural workforce. The California AgTech Alliance helps you identify relevant training partners and career connections, whether you are a student, work in agriculture today, or are considering a move into the field.

See all 93 programs for Workers & Learners

Farmers & Agricultural Operations

Find expertise for technology and workforce decisions on your operation. The Alliance connects growers and agricultural businesses with innovators, educators, and program partners across California. Explore demonstrations, share the challenges a technology needs to address, and connect your team's skills needs with relevant workforce partnerships.

See all 77 programs for Farmers & Agricultural Operations

Industry & Policy Leaders

Help keep agricultural innovation grounded in the needs of California’s farms, businesses, and workforce. The Alliance connects industry experience and public-sector perspectives with research, education, and commercialization partners, creating opportunities to share evidence, identify challenges, and inform collaboration across regions.
