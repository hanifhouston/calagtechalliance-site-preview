Overview

North State–Redwood Coast Ag-Tech Alliance connects agricultural technology, workforce development, and climate-smart practices in Northern California. Its public project plan emphasizes small farms and describes coordinated work on technical assistance, demonstration farms, virtual fencing, irrigation evaluation, and business support. The North State Planning and Development Collective leads coordination with regional research, extension, agriculture, and economic development partners. Farmers, educators, entrepreneurs, and community organizations can approach the coordinating team about the workstream relevant to their needs. Published plans do not establish current intake; confirm rollout and availability directly.

What they do

  • Small agtech business assistanceProvides small agricultural and agtech businesses in the North State with technical assistance such as customer and market research, funding research, websites, and marketing, delivered with regional partners.
  • Regenerative farm demonstrationsSupports a network of mentor farms and demonstration sites, with field days and technical assistance, where Northern California farmers and ranchers see regenerative and climate-smart practices in use.
  • Irrigation efficiencySupports on-farm irrigation system assessments, water-use efficiency tools, and technical support for Siskiyou County producers, delivered by trained irrigation technicians.
  • Regional coordination and trainingLed by the North State Planning and Development Collective, the alliance links research, extension, Tribal, and economic development partners across 14 counties on climate-smart training and workforce capacity.
