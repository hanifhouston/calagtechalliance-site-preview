Overview

UC Davis Venture Catalyst helps researchers and founders turn UC Davis research and technology into startups and real-world applications. Part of UC Davis's Office of Innovation and Economic Development, it supports UC Davis-affiliated founders through its START program, funds proof-of-concept work through STAIR grants, and links early-stage startups with incubator space through the DRIVE network. STEM innovators from UC Davis and across the Central Valley and Sierra can apply to its NSF I-Corps customer-discovery training, which includes an agtech cohort. Eligibility differs by program, so founders and researchers can contact Venture Catalyst to find the right fit.

What they do

  • Research commercializationHelps UC Davis researchers and affiliated founders move inventions, including food and agriculture technologies, toward licensing or a startup with proof-of-concept grants, mentoring, pitch coaching, legal connections, and entrepreneur-in-residence advice.
  • Customer-discovery trainingRuns a regional customer-discovery course, including agtech cohorts, in which technology startup teams from Northern and Central California, Oregon, Washington, and Alaska interview potential customers to test business models.
  • Incubator networkLinks early-stage UC Davis-affiliated startups with a network of themed incubators near campus that offer shared office and lab space.

Programs and services

  • DRIVE Startup Incubator NetworkA network of thematically focused business incubators near the UC Davis campus offering shared office and lab space to early-stage UC Davis-affiliated startups.Program website
  • NSF I-Corps at UC Davis (Regional Customer Discovery Course)A remote customer-discovery course where teams with a technology-based startup idea interview potential customers to test their business model; UC Davis runs agtech-focused cohorts.Program website
  • STAIR Grant ProgramProof-of-concept seed grants, industry mentoring, and entrepreneurial training that help UC Davis researchers move early-stage inventions, including food and agriculture technologies, toward licensing or a startup.Program website
  • START ProgramStartup formation support for UC Davis-affiliated founders commercializing university research, including pitch coaching, grant workshops, legal and service-provider connections, mentors, market reports, and entrepreneur-in-residence advice.Program website
