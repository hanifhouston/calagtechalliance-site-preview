Overview

Napa Valley College is a public community college in Napa whose Viticulture and Winery Technology program prepares students for wine-industry careers and helps current industry employees advance. It offers associate degrees and certificates in viticulture, winemaking, and wine marketing and sales, plus local certificates in job skills such as pest scouting, pruning and wine laboratory work. Students learn hands-on in the college's teaching vineyard and bonded teaching winery and can earn credit for supervised work experience at vineyards and wineries. Prospective students and industry employees can reach the program through its coordinator.

What they do

  • Viticulture educationPrepares students for vineyard careers through degree and certificate study that combines grape-growing coursework with hands-on practice in the college's five-acre teaching vineyard.
  • Winemaking and cellar trainingTrains students for cellar, lab, and production roles, covering winemaking, lab analysis, sensory evaluation, and cellar operations in the college's bonded teaching winery.
  • Wine business and salesTeaches the business side of wine, including marketing, sales, and hospitality, for students and industry employees moving into those roles.
  • Workforce skills and work experienceOffers short job-skill certificates for entry-level and current vineyard and winery workers, and awards college credit for supervised work experience at vineyards and wineries.

Programs and services

  • Vineyard and Winery Local CertificatesShort local certificates, such as Vineyard Pest Scout, Viticulture Operations, and Wine Laboratory Technician, that build specific job skills for entry-level and current vineyard and winery workers.Program website
  • Viticulture & Winery Technology Work Experience (WOEX)Students earn college credit for supervised paid or volunteer work at vineyards and wineries, setting learning objectives with their job supervisor and instructor.Program website
  • Viticulture (A.S.)Associate degree for students preparing for vineyard careers, combining grape-growing coursework with hands-on practice in the college's five-acre teaching vineyard.Program website
  • Viticulture and Enology (Certificate of Achievement)Certificate combining core vineyard and winemaking coursework for students and working professionals who want a focused wine-industry credential without completing a full associate degree.Program website
  • Wine Marketing and Sales (A.S. and Certificate of Achievement)Associate degree and certificate covering the business side of wine, including marketing and sales, for students and wine-industry employees moving into sales, marketing, or hospitality roles.Program website
  • Wine Production (Certificate of Achievement)Certificate of production-oriented courses in viticulture and winemaking, including lab analysis, sensory evaluation, and cellar operations. A distance-education option for working professionals has paired online courses with an on-campus winery intensive.Program website
  • Winemaking (A.S.)Associate degree in winemaking for students preparing for cellar, lab, and production roles, with practical training in the college's bonded teaching winery.Program website
