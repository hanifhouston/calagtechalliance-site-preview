Overview

California Cotton Ginners and Growers Association represents cotton growers and ginners in legislative, regulatory, and industry matters. Its work addresses air and water quality, energy, labor and safety, transportation, and regulations affecting agricultural operations. Growers and agricultural businesses can follow cotton-sector developments, explore member resources, and connect with the association about specific issues. Researchers and educators can use its cotton research information and production-data links, subject to any access requirements. Funding notices require a separate check of current deadlines and eligibility.

What they do

  • Cotton industry advocacyRepresents California cotton growers and ginners on legislative and regulatory matters, including air and water quality, energy, labor and safety, and transportation.
  • Production and research informationShares cotton research information, planting forecasts, and production data used by growers, agricultural businesses, researchers, and educators.
  • Statewide member networkConnects grower, ginner, and associate members across California from its Fresno base, keeping them informed on developments in the cotton sector.
