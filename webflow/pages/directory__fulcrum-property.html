Overview

Fulcrum Property develops, invests in, and operates commercial and multifamily real estate. Its Northern California work includes mixed-use urban development, retail and commercial properties, redevelopment, and housing. Businesses can approach the firm about space needs and potential property fit, while public agencies, economic development organizations, and real estate partners can discuss projects and investment relationships. For agricultural technology organizations, the relevant route is a conversation about facilities, location, and development needs. Availability, leasing terms, and project participation are determined through individual discussions.

What they do

  • Mixed-use and infill developmentDevelops mixed-use urban, infill, redevelopment, and housing projects in the Sacramento region, partnering with public agencies, economic development organizations, and real estate partners.
  • Commercial space for businessesProvides commercial space and facility options for businesses, including agricultural technology organizations looking for a location or facility in the region.
