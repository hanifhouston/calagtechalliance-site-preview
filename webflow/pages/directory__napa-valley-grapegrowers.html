Overview

Napa Valley Grapegrowers supports winegrape growers and vineyard businesses with viticulture information, industry resources, and community connections. Its public materials address growing conditions, soil health, water, pests and diseases, weather, and winegrape markets. Growers and agricultural businesses can also explore membership, networking events, and industry directories. Educators and community organizations can contact the team about vineyard-focused outreach and participation. Some information is public, while membership, event registration, and eligibility depend on the activity; confirm the route that fits your needs.

What they do

  • Viticulture informationPublishes information for winegrape growers on pests and diseases, soil health, water and irrigation, weather, and winegrape markets.
  • Grower and industry networkRepresents and connects Napa Valley winegrape growers and vineyard businesses through membership, advocacy, networking events, and industry directories.
  • Vineyard education and outreachConducts vineyard-focused public education and community engagement with educators and community organizations.
