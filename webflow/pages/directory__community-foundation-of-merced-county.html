Overview

The Community Foundation of Merced County connects local philanthropy with organizations and initiatives serving county residents. Its priorities include education and scholarships, healthcare access, poverty mitigation, and community development. It supports nonprofit organizations and helps donors direct resources toward local needs. The foundation also participates in regional agricultural innovation and workforce conversations through the Merced AgTech Alliance, bringing a community perspective to partnerships among education, industry, and development organizations.

What they do

  • Grants and scholarshipsMakes grants, including general operating support, to nonprofits addressing education, healthcare access, poverty mitigation, and community development in Merced County, and awards scholarships to local students pursuing higher education.
  • Local philanthropyHelps donors direct resources to local needs through endowments, donor-advised funds, scholarship funds, and special initiatives.
  • Regional agtech conveningParticipates in the Merced AgTech Alliance, bringing a community perspective to partnerships among education, industry, and development organizations on agricultural innovation and workforce development.

Programs and services

  • CFMC Scholarships ProgramScholarships that help graduating seniors from Merced County high schools pay for education after high school, including tuition, housing, books and supplies. Awards are usually paid directly to the student's school.Program website
