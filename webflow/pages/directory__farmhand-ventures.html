Overview

Farmhand Ventures invests in early agricultural technology businesses and advises agrifood stakeholders. Its focus is the future of agricultural work and technologies that can support farmworker well-being. Founders can submit businesses for investment consideration, while growers, commodity groups, and other organizations can discuss technology and market questions. Its Commercial Adoption Readiness Framework helps teams examine the conditions needed for an agrifood technology to move toward practical use.

What they do

  • Early-stage agtech investmentInvests at the pre-seed and seed stages in U.S. agricultural technology companies focused on the future of agricultural work and on technologies that support farmworker well-being.
  • Agrifood technology advisingAdvises growers, commodity groups, and other agrifood organizations on technology and market questions. Its adoption readiness framework examines value proposition, market acceptance, resources, and regulatory considerations for new agrifood technologies.
