Overview

Digital NEST helps Latinx youth and other young people from underserved California communities build technology skills, professional networks, and careers. Its programs connect career exploration with technical pathways, individual coaching, and paid work experience. Young people can move through Explore, Build, and Launch activities as they develop skills and prepare for employment. Educators, employers, and community partners can support youth development or connect with BizzNEST’s creative services.

What they do

  • Technology career explorationIntroduces Latinx teens and other young people from underserved communities to design, AI, and coding through hands-on workshops, real projects, and trips to companies.
  • Technical career pathwaysTrains young adults in UX/UI design, digital marketing, web and software development, and information technology, with one-on-one coaching as they build professional portfolios.
  • Paid work experienceRuns an in-house creative agency where young adults work on paid client projects with mentorship, resume coaching, and interview preparation; the agency also provides creative services to businesses and organizations.

Programs and services

  • Build Career PathwaysA year-long pathway in UX/UI design, digital marketing, web and software development, or IT where young adults build a professional portfolio with one-on-one coaching.Program website
  • Explore (Quest Club and Tech Studios)Career exploration for teens at Digital NEST centers, with hands-on design, AI, and coding workshops, real projects, and company visits that help members discover their interests.Program website
  • Launch at BizzNESTYoung adults apply to BizzNEST, Digital NEST's in-house creative agency, to work on paid client projects with mentorship, resume coaching, and interview preparation.Program website
