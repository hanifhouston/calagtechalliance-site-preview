Overview

Western Growers supports the fresh produce industry through advocacy, member services, education, and agricultural innovation. Its practical resources address the operating challenges of growers and agricultural businesses, including workforce management, food safety, and technology adoption.

Programs and services

  • California Agricultural Workforce Development ProgramAlliance programConnects agricultural employers, community colleges, and learners around technology skills, curriculum, training, and work experience.Program website
  • Western Growers Center for Innovation & TechnologyConnections between agricultural technology companies, growers, and shippers for feedback and field testing.Program website
  • Western Growers UniversityProfessional development covering leadership, supervisory skills, employment law, safety, and food safety.Program website
