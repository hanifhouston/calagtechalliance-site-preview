Overview

The City of Woodland’s economic development work includes a focus on food and agriculture. Through The Food Front, the city highlights local producers and businesses and helps connect agricultural production, food manufacturing, and innovation activity. Businesses can explore local projects and ask about regional relationships, while community and education partners can find information about Woodland’s food economy. The city also provides a route to discuss food-system and business-development collaboration. Partner-operated innovation facilities have their own access requirements and should be contacted directly.

What they do

  • Food and agriculture economyThrough The Food Front, the city highlights Woodland's food producers and businesses and connects agricultural production, food manufacturing, and innovation activity, including partner-run innovation facilities.
  • Regional innovation connectionsConnects Woodland's food and agriculture businesses with the greater Sacramento agricultural innovation community, and shares public information on the city's food economy and community food projects.
  • Entrepreneurship trainingOffers aspiring business owners in Yolo County a course on business fundamentals, marketing, finance, and permits, paired with ongoing coaching and access to a library makerspace.

Programs and services
