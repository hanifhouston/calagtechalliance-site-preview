Overview

Epix Studios helps organizations communicate social and environmental impact through design, websites, film, and content. It works with mission-driven businesses, foundations, nonprofits, universities, and public institutions, connecting communication strategy with the production of materials for customers, supporters, and other audiences. Services range from brand and website projects to reports, visual explanations, and campaign films. Organizations can discuss a tailored project or ongoing creative subscription, with scope, deliverables, schedule, and budget agreed directly with the studio.

What they do

  • Brand and website designDesigns brand identities, presentation materials, websites, and landing pages that help mission-driven organizations explain their products and their social or environmental impact.
  • Film and visual storytellingProduces documentary and campaign films, infographics, illustrations, newsletters, and scripts that explain complex work to specific audiences.
  • Impact communicationsCreates impact and annual reports, donor communications, and campaign materials for foundations, nonprofits, universities, public institutions, and mission-driven businesses, through tailored projects or an ongoing creative subscription.
