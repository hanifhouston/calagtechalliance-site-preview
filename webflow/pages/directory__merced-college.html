Overview

Merced College connects agricultural education with industrial and technical skills. Its competency-based AgTEC Agriculture Systems Technology certificate prepares students for careers in a changing agricultural industry through practical work and systems learning. The School of Agriculture and Industrial Technology also offers agriculture, mechanized agriculture, diesel technology, industrial maintenance, welding, and related pathways. Students, employers, and education partners can connect with these programs to discuss training, enrollment, or agricultural workforce collaboration.

What they do

  • Agriculture degrees and transferOffers associate, transfer, and certificate programs in agriculture business, animal, crop, and plant science, horticulture, and agriculture education, taught on the college farm and in greenhouses and livestock facilities.
  • Agricultural technologyTrains farmworkers and other agricultural learners in agtech skills through a noncredit, competency-based certificate with bilingual support, and has proposed a bachelor's degree in agricultural technology.
  • Equipment and technical tradesTrains students, working farmers, and technicians on agricultural equipment, diesel engines, hydraulics, and power equipment, alongside related pathways in industrial maintenance and welding.
  • Student leadership in agricultureDevelops agriculture students' communication and leadership skills as they plan events, lead tours, and give presentations promoting agriculture education.

Programs and services

  • Agricultural AmbassadorsA leadership program for Merced College agriculture students. Ambassadors build communication and leadership skills, plan events, lead tours and give presentations promoting agriculture education.Program website
  • Agriculture degrees and certificatesAssociate, transfer and certificate programs in agriculture business, animal science, crop and plant science, horticulture, equine science and agriculture education, with hands-on learning on the college farm, greenhouses and livestock facilities.Program website
  • AgSTEP Agriculture Systems CertificateA noncredit, competency-based community college certificate in which farmworkers and other agricultural learners build on skills they already have and gain agtech skills, with bilingual support and stackable pathways toward degrees.Program website
  • Bachelor of Science in Applied Agricultural Technology Systems (proposed)A proposed bachelor's degree that would combine agricultural technology and food systems education with applied workforce skills. It would be Merced College's first bachelor's degree.Program website
  • Mechanized Agriculture TechnologyAn associate degree and certificates in agricultural equipment, diesel engines, hydraulics and power equipment. It prepares students for technician jobs and helps working farmers and technicians update their skills.Program website
