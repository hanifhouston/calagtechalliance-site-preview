Overview

Merced AgTech Alliance brings education, research, industry, and community partners together around agricultural innovation and local economic opportunity. Its connected work includes Merced College workforce pathways, UC Merced research and development, and planned startup infrastructure through Reservoir Farms. The Community Foundation of Merced County helps convene partners and connect community priorities with this activity. Growers, educators, and technology businesses can ask for the appropriate partner referral. Announced facilities remain subject to development and readiness checks; current programs, research access, internships, and startup intake should be confirmed with their individual providers.

What they do

  • Regional partner networkBrings education, research, industry, and community partners together around agricultural innovation and local economic opportunity, with the Community Foundation of Merced County helping convene partners.
  • Agtech startup incubationPlans startup infrastructure through partner Reservoir Farms, whose working-farm incubators let agtech and robotics startups test products on farmed plots with grower introductions, advising, workspace, and investor exposure.
  • Agricultural workforce pathwaysConnects students with agricultural technology companies and work experience through Merced College workforce pathways.
  • University research and developmentDraws on agricultural research and development at UC Merced, including a planned facility for agtech research.
