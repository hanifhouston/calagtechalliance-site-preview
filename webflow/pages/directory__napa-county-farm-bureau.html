Overview

Napa County Farm Bureau represents farmers, ranchers, and agricultural businesses through advocacy, education, member resources, and community outreach. Growers can follow legislative and labor-compliance information, explore webinars and discounts, and connect with the local agricultural community. Young Farmers and Ranchers activities and youth programs provide additional routes into agriculture education. Public agencies and community organizations can discuss outreach and industry connections with the team. Membership options, dues, event registration, and individual program requirements vary, so prospective participants should review the relevant route before joining.

What they do

  • Agricultural advocacy and member resourcesRepresents Napa County farmers, ranchers, and agricultural businesses on policy, and provides members with legislative updates, labor-compliance information, webinars, and discounts, linking local growers to state and federal advocacy.
  • Vineyard water-quality complianceRuns group enrollment and water-quality monitoring that helps Napa River watershed vineyards meet the regional water board's vineyard permit requirements.
  • Youth and young farmer supportSupports young people entering agriculture through college scholarships for future agriculture students, loans for 4-H, FFA, and Grange animal and vocational projects, and Young Farmers and Ranchers activities.
  • Community outreachConducts agriculture-focused outreach and connects public agencies and community organizations with Napa's farming community.

Programs and services

  • Aldo Delfino Memorial ScholarshipScholarships from the Napa County Farm Bureau Foundation for high school seniors who plan to study agriculture in college.Program website
  • FFA & 4-H LoansLow- or no-interest loans, offered with F&M Bank, that help Napa County 4-H, FFA and Grange youth cover start-up costs for animal and vocational projects.Program website
  • Region 2 Vineyard Permit Group ProgramGroup enrollment that helps Napa River watershed vineyards meet the regional water board's Vineyard General Permit. The Farm Bureau collects fees and runs group water-quality monitoring for enrolled properties.Program website
