Overview

F3 Innovate connects workforce development, technology commercialization, and investment to strengthen agriculture and rural economies. Based in California's Central Valley, it works with industry, government, and research partners to move agricultural innovations toward field validation and adoption. Its Talent Development programs connect students and educators with agricultural technology, while the Commercialization Engine supports technical and business development. The Catalyst Fund is another current program area; eligibility, intake, and support are determined by each program.

What they do

  • Technology commercializationConnects growers and agtech companies through grower challenge workshops, an R&D library, market-entry preparation, and technology transfer support, helping field-ready technologies move toward adoption on farms.
  • Agtech validation fundingAwards competitive grants with mentorship and pilot coordination to teams field-testing agricultural technologies with Central Valley growers, and provides outcome-based working capital for putting proven technologies into use at scale.
  • Agricultural AI and dataProvides regional supercomputing and shared Central Valley agricultural datasets for startups and researchers, and runs AI sprints in which student, researcher, and independent teams tackle problems defined by growers.
  • Student talent developmentIntroduces middle school, high school, and community college students to agtech through AI training and funded science projects, and places undergraduate and graduate students in paid internships with Central Valley organizations.

Programs and services

  • AI LaunchpadAn applied AI, data science, and cloud computing program for high school and community college students, built on Central Valley weather, soil, and crop data and leading into F3i Data Challenges.Program website
  • Commercialization EngineConnects growers and agtech companies through grower challenge workshops, an R&D library, market-entry preparation, and technology transfer support, helping field-ready solutions move toward adoption on farms.Program website
  • F3 Innovate Catalyst FundOutcome-based working capital, such as recoverable grants or revenue sharing, that helps growers, integrators, and technology providers test and put proven agricultural technologies into use at scale.Program website
  • F3i Supercomputing CenterRegional AI and data-processing infrastructure for agricultural modeling and research, giving California agtech startups and researchers access to computing capacity and shared agricultural data through an online portal.Program website
  • Ignition GrantsProject funding and mentorship for middle and high school students building science fair projects on agriculture and food systems challenges, from research through a working prototype, guided by a science fair coach.Program website
  • Innovation GrantsCompetitive grants with mentorship, pilot coordination, and commercialization support for teams ready to field-test and validate agricultural technologies with growers and operators in the Central Valley.Program website
  • TerraTech Summer InternshipA paid summer internship placing undergraduate and graduate students with Central Valley companies, startups, nonprofits, and public agencies on food and agriculture projects in AI, data science, automation, robotics, and sustainability.Program website
