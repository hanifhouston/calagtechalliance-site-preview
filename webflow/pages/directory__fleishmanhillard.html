Overview

FleishmanHillard advises organizations on communications, reputation, and public engagement. Its Sacramento office focuses on food, beverage, and agribusiness communications, bringing sector knowledge to consumer marketing, media relations, and corporate storytelling. Agricultural businesses, startups, associations, and public partners can discuss how to explain their work, reach audiences, or manage communications around sensitive issues. The firm also connects clients with teams in other markets through its international network. Services are professional client engagements with scope and fees agreed directly.

  • Issues and crisis communicationsAdvises organizations on stakeholder messaging and reputation around sensitive issues, and provides communications training tailored to each organization.
  • International networkConnects clients with communications teams in other markets through offices and affiliates across North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, and Asia.
