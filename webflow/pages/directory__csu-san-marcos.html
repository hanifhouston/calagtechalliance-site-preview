Overview

California State University San Marcos provides university education and research in North San Diego County. Its College of Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics connects classroom instruction with applied learning across biological sciences, biotechnology, chemistry, computing, engineering, mathematics, and physics. These disciplines support skills relevant to agricultural technology, including software, sensors, and biological systems. Students can explore degrees and career resources, while educators, employers, and research partners can approach the college about STEM outreach and collaboration. Admission and participation requirements depend on the relevant program.

What they do

  • STEM degreesOffers bachelor's and master's degrees in biological sciences, biotechnology, chemistry, computer science, engineering, mathematics, and physics, building skills used in agricultural technology such as software, sensors, and biological systems.
  • Undergraduate research and mentoringPairs STEM undergraduates with industry professionals for career mentoring, and offers paid research experience with faculty mentors to CSUSM and local community college students.
  • Entrepreneurship trainingSupports aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs, with a focus on underinvested communities, through a business bootcamp, one-on-one mentoring, and help from business students.
  • STEM outreach and partnershipsRuns STEM outreach for young people and educators in North County, and connects employers and research partners with faculty expertise and its partnership network.

Programs and services

  • CSTEM Professional Mentoring ProgramPairs CSUSM STEM undergraduates with industry professionals for monthly mentoring on careers, interviews, resumes, networking, and graduate school.Program website
  • CSTEM Summer Scholars ProgramPaid summer research experience where CSUSM and local community college undergraduates work with a faculty mentor on STEM projects, with weekly seminars and a closing poster showcase.Program website
  • Resilient Entrepreneur ProgramA multi-month program for aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs combining a Saturday business bootcamp, one-on-one mentoring, and support from business students, with a focus on underinvested communities.Program website
