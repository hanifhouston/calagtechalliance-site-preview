Overview

RIoT Labs helps entrepreneurs and organizations develop, test, and bring emerging technologies to market. Working within the Wireless Research Center, it connects innovators with technical expertise and commercialization partners. Its agricultural work includes The Source, a smart agriculture testbed in Wilson, North Carolina. Greenhouses and high tunnels give growers, technology developers, researchers, and educators a place to explore crops, growing methods, and agricultural technologies. Project and facility support depends on fit, eligibility, scheduling, and costs.

What they do

  • Smart agriculture testbedRuns a greenhouse and high-tunnel testbed in Wilson, North Carolina, where agtech startups, growers, and researchers trial sensors, AI, data tools, autonomous equipment, crops, and growing methods in real conditions.
  • Connected-device engineeringProvides prototyping, technology validation, radio-frequency testing, and device certification support for connected-device startups, including agtech companies, through the Wireless Research Center.
  • Technology learning resourcesShares educational resources and recordings on connected technology, AI, and business development, and arranges hands-on smart agriculture learning through institutional partners.
