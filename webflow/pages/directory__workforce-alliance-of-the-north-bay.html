Overview

Workforce Alliance of the North Bay connects career seekers, employers, education providers, and economic development partners. Its CareerPoint network provides employment and training services, while business resources help employers recruit and develop their workforce. Regional labor-market information and career exposure resources support planning and education partnerships. People and organizations in Lake, Marin, Mendocino, and Napa counties can contact the appropriate regional center or business-services team to discuss their needs.

What they do

  • Career servicesCareer centers in Lake, Marin, Mendocino, and Napa counties offer career coaching, job search help, workshops, hiring events, and referrals to training programs.
  • Employer hiring and trainingHelps North Bay employers recruit through candidate referrals, recruiting events, job fairs, and postings, and supports on-the-job training, upskilling, and customized training developed with regional education providers.
  • Youth careers and workforce dataIntroduces high school students to careers through volunteer professionals and career path tools, and provides regional labor-market information for educators, employers, and economic development partners.
  • Wildfire resilience trainingTrains Lake County residents for wildland firefighting and fire and forestry careers, covering fuels reduction, land restoration, chainsaw operation, and cultural fire practices, with paid field training.

Programs and services

  • CareerPoint Business Services: Hire Qualified TalentRecruiting help for North Bay businesses, including pre-screened candidate referrals, on-site recruiting events, job fairs, job postings, and answers to employment questions.Program website
  • CareerPoint Employer Training Services (On-the-Job, Incumbent Worker and Customized Training)Training support for North Bay employers, including on-the-job training with wage reimbursement, upskilling for current employees, and customized training developed with regional education providers.Program website
  • CareerPoint North Bay Career CentersCareer centers in Lake, Marin, Mendocino and Napa counties offering career coaching, job search help, workshops, hiring events and referrals to training programs.Program website
  • CareerPoint Youth ServicesCareer exposure for high school students through a network of volunteer professionals and career path tools, plus youth employment services through the CareerPoint centers.Program website
  • Lake Wildfire Resilience Workforce ProgramWildland firefighter certification, fire and forestry career training, and paid on-the-job field training for Lake County residents. Training covers fuels reduction, land restoration, chainsaw operation and cultural fire practices.Program website
