Overview

F3 Local is a regional initiative led by UC Agriculture and Natural Resources (UC ANR) to build a more inclusive and resilient food economy in California's San Joaquin Valley. It connects small-scale farmers and food entrepreneurs with practical tools, training and markets. Farmers in Fresno, Kings, Madera, Merced and Tulare counties can borrow specialized equipment from its lending library at UC ANR's Kearney Agricultural Research and Extension Center, get one-on-one guidance on affordable technology, and trial new practices with researchers through SF-ADAPT. Food entrepreneurs, especially mobile food vendors and early-stage businesses, can get training and advising on licensing, permits, food safety, marketing and distribution. Farmers and food businesses can contact the F3 Local team in Fresno to find the right support.

What they do

  • Small-farm technology accessLends scale-appropriate farm equipment, with training, so small farms can try technology before buying, and runs a competition that awards cash prizes for practical hardware, software, and social solutions for small farms.
  • On-farm research and trialsConnects small and medium-scale farmers with UC researchers and technology developers to co-create tools and run field trials and demonstrations, and offers hands-on training on irrigation, soil health, and crop management.
  • Food business developmentTrains and coaches aspiring and growing food entrepreneurs, especially mobile food vendors and cottage food operators, on business planning, licensing, permits, food safety, product development, and funding.
  • Local food marketsRaises the visibility of local food businesses through regional branding and promotion, and aggregates products from small farms and food businesses for coordinated distribution to schools, hospitals, and grocers.

Programs and services

  • Equipment Lending LibraryShort-term loans of specialized, scale-appropriate farm tools and equipment from the Kearney research center, with required training and staff delivery, so small Central Valley farms can try technology before buying.Program website
  • Farm Research and InnovationConnects Central Valley growers with UC researchers and product developers to run field trials, share farmer feedback with developers, and deliver hands-on training on irrigation, soil health, and crop management.Program website
  • Food Entrepreneurship Academy (Business Launchpad)Training and coaching on business planning, licensing, food safety, and permitting for aspiring food entrepreneurs, especially mobile food vendors and cottage food operators, with connections to shared kitchens.Program website
  • Local Food Marketing AssistanceMarketing support that raises the visibility of local food businesses through regional branding, pop-ups, social media campaigns, and introductions to retailers and institutions.Program website
  • Small Farm Food Aggregation HubsAggregation hubs that combine products from small farms and food businesses for coordinated distribution to schools, hospitals, and grocers, helping producers reach institutional and wholesale buyers.Program website
  • Wrap Around Business ServicesOne-on-one consulting for food entrepreneurs on licensing and permits, product development, food safety compliance, marketing, and funding navigation, with continued support as businesses grow.Program website
