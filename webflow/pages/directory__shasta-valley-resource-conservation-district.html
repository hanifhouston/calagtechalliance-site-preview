Overview

Shasta Valley Resource Conservation District works with landowners, agricultural producers, Tribes, and public agencies on land stewardship and watershed health. Its programs address soil health, water resources, forest restoration, and prescribed fire. Farmers can ask about Healthy Soils technical assistance, while conservation and community partners can discuss monitoring, restoration, and collaborative projects. Based in Yreka, the district serves central Siskiyou County and undertakes project work across the county. Assistance depends on location, program requirements, and available resources.

What they do

  • Soil health assistanceProvides technical assistance to Siskiyou County farmers and ranchers in a state-funded soil health grant program, from grant applications through the design and implementation of soil health practices.
  • Water and watershed stewardshipSupports watershed monitoring, groundwater studies, and restoration with landowners, researchers, and agencies. Two state weather stations it set up with DWR supply evapotranspiration data that local growers use to schedule irrigation.
  • Forest and fire stewardshipSupports forest restoration, fuel reduction, and prescribed burning, including co-sponsoring a community burn association that provides consultations, equipment, and hands-on help for Siskiyou County landowners.
  • Technical consultingDistrict staff consult for producers, landowners, and agencies on agricultural management and business, forestry, geology, grant writing, project development, environmental review, and water monitoring.

Programs and services

  • Fee-for-Service ConsultingPaid technical support from district staff in agricultural management and business, forestry, geology, grant writing, project development, environmental review, and water monitoring, available on request to producers, landowners, and agencies.Program website
  • Healthy Soils Program Technical Assistance (Siskiyou County)No-cost technical assistance for Siskiyou County farmers and ranchers in a state-funded Healthy Soils grant program, from application support through design and implementation of soil health practices.Program website
  • Siskiyou Prescribed Burn AssociationCommunity prescribed burn association, co-sponsored by the district, that offers burn consultations, equipment, and help conducting prescribed burns for Siskiyou County landowners, and has offered public burn trainings.Program website
