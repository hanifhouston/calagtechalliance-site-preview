Overview

Foundation for California Community Colleges, also known as FoundationCCC, supports students, colleges, and communities across California. Its workforce portfolio helps education and employer partners build employment pathways, manage paid work experience, and expand apprenticeships. It also works through local partners on training and employment support for eligible participants. Services are delivered through colleges, employers, public agencies, workforce intermediaries, and nonprofit organizations, so access depends on the specific program and partner arrangements. Education and workforce organizations can approach the relevant team about agreements, technical assistance, or collaboration.

What they do

  • Workforce pathways and apprenticeshipsHelps colleges and employers build employment pathways and expand apprenticeships through technical assistance, communities of practice, and regional education-to-workforce collaboration.
  • Paid work experience administrationServes as employer of record for internships and paid work experience, handling payroll, workers' compensation, and human resources for education and employer partners.
  • Farmworker agtech educationPartners with community colleges on a noncredit, competency-based agtech certificate that lets farmworkers build on skills they already have, with bilingual support and stackable pathways toward degrees.
  • Community employment supportWorks through partner colleges and organizations to provide training and employment support to CalFresh participants and students with disabilities, and administers grants for programs helping underserved people find and keep work.

Programs and services

  • Fresh SuccessEducation, training and employment support for CalFresh participants, delivered by participating community colleges, adult schools and community-based organizations and administered by the Foundation for California Community Colleges. Services can include classes, career counseling, job search and placement help, tutoring, case management, and help with costs such as transportation and textbooks.Program website
  • Student Training & Employment Program (STEP)Paid work experience and job preparation for California students with disabilities ages 16 to 21, coordinated by the California Department of Rehabilitation and the Foundation for California Community Colleges and delivered through local workforce development boards. Students build workplace readiness and self-advocacy skills, explore careers and get counseling on education after high school.Program website
