Overview

The Bay Area Council brings employers and civic leaders together to address regional economic and community challenges. Its work combines policy research, business engagement, and advocacy on workforce, housing, transportation, innovation, and climate. Agricultural technology organizations can connect with the Bay Area's wider business and innovation economy through its employer networks, workforce discussions, and international engagement. Membership and program arrangements determine participation in committees and other activities.

What they do

  • Regional policy and advocacyBrings employers and civic leaders together on policy research and advocacy addressing Bay Area workforce, housing, transportation, innovation, and climate challenges.
  • Employer workforce collaborationConvenes employers on apprenticeships, work-based learning, skills-based hiring, and talent needs through a member committee focused on the future workforce.
  • International business connectionsLinks the Bay Area with markets in Europe, China, India, and the Americas through business delegations, meetings with business and government leaders from abroad, and research on international economic relationships.
