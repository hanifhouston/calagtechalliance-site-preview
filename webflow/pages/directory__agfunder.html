Overview

AgFunder invests in technology companies working across agriculture and food, with interests spanning artificial intelligence, biology, climate, and the modernization of agrifood systems. It combines selective venture investment with industry networks and support for the companies it backs. Entrepreneurs, growers, industry organizations, and investors can also use its public research and publications to understand companies, market trends, and investment activity. Funding decisions and portfolio support follow the firm's investment process.
