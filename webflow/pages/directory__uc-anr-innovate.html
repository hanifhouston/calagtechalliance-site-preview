Overview

UC ANR Innovate is the innovation arm of UC Agriculture and Natural Resources. It connects research, entrepreneurs and growers around agricultural technology development, field validation, commercialization, workforce learning and regional collaboration. Its portfolio includes UC ANR Build, UC ANR Connect, Farm Robotics Challenge and Farm Robotics Academy.
