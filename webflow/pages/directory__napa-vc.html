Overview

Napa VC describes an early-stage investment focus on wine, hospitality, robotics, and data intelligence. It pairs capital with founder support in recruiting, market entry, and follow-on financing for companies it backs. Technology startups working on automation, analytics, or related wine and hospitality applications can contact the firm to discuss investment fit. Its stated geographic interests include Napa and the broader West Coast. Investment is selective, and the public website does not establish an open funding application or grant program.
