Overview

The California Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development (GO-Biz) helps businesses start, locate, retain, and expand operations in California. It provides no-cost consultation and connects business needs with public agencies and business-assistance organizations. Agricultural businesses and technology companies can use GO-Biz to navigate location decisions, permitting questions, incentives, and small-business resources. Regional partners can also access information for economic development planning. Requirements and eligibility depend on the specific program or agency involved.

What they do

  • Business location and expansionProvides confidential help to companies and international investors locating, expanding, or staying in California, covering site selection, incentive navigation, and connections to state, local, and utility partners.
  • Small business and startup supportSupports statewide networks of small business centers and inclusive innovation hubs that give entrepreneurs, including underrepresented founders, advising, training, workspace, and connections to lenders and innovation grants.
  • Incentives, financing, and permitsAdministers a competitive state tax credit for businesses that commit to creating jobs in California, offers small farm loans through partner lenders, and guides businesses through permits, licenses, and compliance.
  • Export promotionHelps California small businesses, including farms, reach export markets through trade show pavilions, trade missions, export vouchers, and market-entry training.

Programs and services

  • California Business Investment Services (CalBIS)Confidential help for companies locating, expanding, or staying in California, including site selection, incentive navigation, and connections to state, local, and utility partners.Program website
  • California Competes Tax CreditCompetitive state income tax credit for businesses of any industry or size that commit to creating full-time jobs and investing in California, with GO-Biz staff available for application consultations.Program website
  • California State Trade Expansion Program (STEP)Export assistance for California small businesses, including California pavilions at international trade shows and missions, export vouchers, and export training such as sector-focused market-entry programs.Program website
  • IBank Farm Loan ProgramSmall farm loans made through participating Financial Development Corporations and backed by USDA Farm Service Agency guarantees, for small farms and ranches in California.Program website
  • Invest in California (foreign direct investment services)Support for international companies and investors weighing California operations, including site selection help, wage, tax, and utility cost data, and networking with state and regional partners.Program website
  • Permit and Regulatory Assistance (including CalGold)Guidance for businesses of any size on registering, identifying required federal, state, regional, and local permits and licenses, and staying in compliance, backed by the CalGold permit lookup tool.Program website
  • SCALE Small Business Centers NetworkStatewide network of small business centers offering no-cost one-on-one advising, low-cost training, and help connecting with lenders, in person or virtually.Program website
