Overview

Napa County Hispanic Chamber of Commerce supports Hispanic businesses, professionals, and entrepreneurs through business connections, advocacy, and leadership. Its networking events, member connections, and business resources provide ways to engage with the local business community. Entrepreneurs and regional partners can explore membership or events and discuss opportunities to collaborate with Hispanic business leaders. The Chamber’s documented mission and service community are centered on Napa County.

What they do

  • Business networking and resourcesHosts mixers and events and shares business resources that connect Hispanic businesses, professionals, and entrepreneurs with one another and with Napa County's wider business community.
  • Business advocacy and leadershipProvides Hispanic business leaders in Napa County with a forum for business advocacy, leadership, and local collaboration.
