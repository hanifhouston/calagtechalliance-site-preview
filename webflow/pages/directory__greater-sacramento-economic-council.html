Overview

The Greater Sacramento Economic Council helps companies establish, retain, and expand operations in the Sacramento region. It combines business assistance, regional research, and connections to public agencies, utilities, and workforce partners. Companies can discuss location, operating costs, hiring, and market-entry needs with its business development team. Its regional analytics and collaborative work also help economic development and education partners understand business priorities. Relocation, expansion, and retention assistance is described as complimentary; the support available depends on the project.

What they do

  • Business location and expansionHelps companies, including agri-food tech firms, move to, expand in, or stay in the six-county Sacramento region through site selection, incentive navigation, operating-cost research, and partner introductions.
  • International business attractionSupports international companies entering or expanding in Greater Sacramento with site selection, incentives, research, and connections to consulates, service providers, and international investors.
  • Workforce connectionsIntroduces employers to staffing agencies, higher education institutions, and training partners to address hiring and skill needs.
  • Regional economic researchProduces regional analytics and works with jurisdictions, industry leaders, and economic development and education partners to understand business priorities and support regional growth.
