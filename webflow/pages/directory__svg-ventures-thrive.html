Overview

SVG Ventures | THRIVE supports agrifood technology businesses through investment, startup programs, and corporate innovation partnerships. Its programming addresses idea development, business validation, growth, and entry into new markets. Researchers and founders can explore programs matched to their stage and region, while established businesses can discuss technology scouting and collaboration with startup teams. Investors and ecosystem partners can approach THRIVE about investment, mentoring, and partnership opportunities. Admission, terms, and deadlines vary by program, corporate services use tailored arrangements, and funding depends on selective investment decisions.

What they do

  • Early-stage venture buildingHelps researchers and first-time founders turn agrifood tech ideas into minimum viable products and validated businesses through customer discovery, expert advice, and cohort learning.
  • Acceleration and market entryRuns a global agrifood tech startup competition, an accelerator for growth-stage agrifood, climate, and energy tech companies, and market-entry support that brings international agritech companies to Silicon Valley.
  • Venture investmentInvests in seed to Series A agrifood tech startups across biotech, food, supply chain, digital farming, and sustainability through its venture fund.
  • Corporate innovation servicesWorks with established agrifood businesses on startup scouting, technology research, innovation planning, workshops, and evaluating or managing trials with startup partners.

Programs and services

  • SVG Ventures Pioneer FundSVG Ventures fund that invests in seed to Series A agrifood tech startups across biotech, food, supply chain, digital farming and sustainability.Program website
  • THRIVE Global Impact Accelerator ProgramAccelerator for Series A+ agrifood, climate and energy tech companies chosen from top Global Impact Challenge applicants. It offers virtual programming, mentorship and an in-person week in Silicon Valley.Program website
  • THRIVE Studio SprintSix-week virtual program that helps researchers and first-time founders take an agrifood tech idea to a defined minimum viable product through customer discovery, expert advice and learning alongside a cohort.Program website
