Overview

CITRIS at UC Merced connects technology with agricultural and community needs through drone education, remote sensing, and agricultural research. CITRIS FLY works with schools and community organizations on activities building programming, mission planning, spatial awareness, and critical thinking. Educators can also explore the DUCC drone-training interest route. Its agriculture portfolio links to the Experimental Smart Farm, where research proposals can involve plots, sensors, data infrastructure, and field support. Research access requires university review, scheduling, safety preparation, and relevant agreements.

What they do

  • Youth drone and robotics educationBrings drone activities to Central Valley schools and community organizations, and teaches San Joaquin Valley middle and high school students robotics and coding with UC Merced near-peer mentors and agtech research examples.
  • Drone educator trainingHosts a regional drone-training hub for high school and community college educators across the northern and central San Joaquin Valley, Kern County, and neighboring Sierra and inland counties.
  • Smart farm researchSupports agricultural technology and remote-sensing research at an experimental smart farm, where research projects use plots, sensors, data infrastructure, and field support.

Programs and services

  • Drones Uplifting California Communities (DUCC)Alliance programEducator training that expands access to drone skills and education through schools and colleges across California.Program website
  • NexTech RoboticsA free robotics and coding program for San Joaquin Valley middle and high school students, taught by UC Merced near-peer mentors and using real ag-tech research case studies to show robotics in agriculture.Program website
