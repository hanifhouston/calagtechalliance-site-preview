Overview

Uplift Central Coast brings residents, businesses, workforce partners, public agencies, and community organizations together to develop an inclusive and resilient regional economy. Through California Jobs First, its work combines community engagement, economic research, regional planning, and support for developing investment-ready projects. Partners can discuss workforce and industry priorities, contribute local perspectives, and explore published regional research. Organizations developing projects can ask about current predevelopment or implementation opportunities. Funding eligibility and application status must be checked with the team; earlier award announcements do not establish an open funding round.

What they do

  • Regional economic planningBrings residents, businesses, workforce partners, public agencies, and community organizations across six Central Coast counties together to plan an inclusive, resilient economy, with subregional conveners gathering local priorities in each county.
  • Economic research and dataPublishes regional plans, economic research, and data that inform workforce, industry, and project planning on the Central Coast.
  • Project development supportSupports organizations developing investment-ready regional projects through California Jobs First, from predevelopment through implementation.
