Overview

Uplift Central Coast is a six-county coalition convened by REACH, the Monterey Bay Economic Partnership (MBEP), and the Economic Development Collaborative (EDC).

The Uplift Central Coast coalition began as an opportunity to organize a six-county region under California Jobs First, working together at a regional scale to attract investment and expand opportunities for Central Coast residents. By directly engaging the people best suited to help a community prosper (residents, businesses, workforce partners, public agencies, and community organizations), Uplift created a forward-looking plan to expand access to quality jobs, drive new regional investment, and work to create a more inclusive economy.

What Uplift set out to do. The six-county Uplift Central Coast region developed a community economic development plan focused on the unique priorities and opportunities of the Central Coast. Through state funding and support under the California Jobs First umbrella, Uplift Central Coast identified opportunities to maximize the impact of available resources and established a framework for investing in the region’s economic future.

How we brought it to life. By working directly with local stakeholders and inviting traditionally underserved parties to the table – as well as experts in job development, workforce training, housing, financial services, enterprise development and other fields critical to economic growth.

Why do we do it? The Central Coast is rich with talent, opportunity and assets that can help communities build resilience and create lasting prosperity. Uplift Central Coast recognized that those best positioned to strengthen a community are the people who live, work, and invest in its future. By bringing six counties together around shared priorities, the initiative helped elevate the visibility of the region as a whole and build greater awareness of the Central Coast’s collective potential.

For programs and services, see the three co-conveners: REACH, Monterey Bay Economic Partnership and Economic Development Collaborative.
