For service providers

Put your expertise to work advancing AgTech innovation.

Support startup projects with your expertise, facilities or land. UC ANR pays participating providers for approved services under the applicable voucher agreement.

Providers need demonstrated experience within or directly supporting California’s agricultural or AgTech value chain, plus the capacity to deliver services in at least one category. All voucher-funded services must be performed in California.

Service categories

Review the categories you can offer.

01 · Land Services

Field environments for testing and validation

Established California farms, ranches, Research and Extension Centers, agricultural research facilities or other sites with demonstrated capacity for field trials, demonstrations, testing or validation may qualify.

02 · Infrastructure and Technology Services

Facilities and technical capabilities

Providers need experience supporting AgTech companies or entrepreneurs, or conducting relevant agricultural R&D in the California market, plus access to the facilities, equipment, software or expertise needed for the work.

03 · Professional Services

Experience helping AgTech businesses advance

Providers must have supported at least five AgTech companies or entrepreneurs within the past two years through incubation, acceleration, technical assistance, business development, commercialization or similar services.

You may offer more than one category if you meet the qualifications for each.
