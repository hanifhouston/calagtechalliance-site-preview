Overview

Limagrain breeds, produces, and markets seeds and operates related agrifood businesses. Its group includes field and vegetable seeds, garden products, cereal ingredients, bakery products, and a farmer cooperative. The cooperative gives farmers a role in group governance, while individual businesses serve different crops, customers, and markets. Growers, researchers, and industry partners can use the business-line and innovation routes to identify the team relevant to their product or collaboration needs.

What they do

  • Field and vegetable seedsBreeds, produces, and markets field and vegetable seeds through businesses that serve different crops, customers, and markets around the world.
  • Plant breeding and researchCarries out plant breeding, agricultural research, and innovation through an international research network linked to its crop-specific businesses.
  • Cereal, bakery, and garden productsBeyond seeds, group businesses produce cereal ingredients, bakery products, and garden products for a range of customers and markets.
  • Farmer cooperativeThe group, rooted in France's Auvergne region, includes a farmer cooperative that gives farmers a role in its governance.
