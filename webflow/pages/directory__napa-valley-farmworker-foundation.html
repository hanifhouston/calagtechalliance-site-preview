Overview

Napa Valley Farmworker Foundation supports vineyard workers through education, professional development, and community resources. Its programs address literacy, vineyard skills and safety, leadership, mentorship, and family needs. Farmworkers and agricultural employees can explore classes that support workplace and personal development, while students and emerging professionals can check mentorship and scholarship pathways connected to vineyard careers. Community partners can also discuss ways to support farmworker education. Registration, eligibility, languages, and schedules depend on the individual program.

What they do

  • Adult literacyTeaches English, math, financial, and computer literacy to Napa County farmworkers and their families, with classes scheduled around vineyard work.
  • Vineyard skills and safetyTrains vineyard workers in pruning, canopy management, irrigation, and vineyard technology, along with hands-on safety training in first aid, equipment, pesticides, and night-harvest protocols.
  • Leadership and management developmentDevelops emerging vineyard leaders and supervisors in communication, decision-making, and day-to-day management, and runs workshops on teamwork and engagement for vineyard crews.
  • Mentorship and scholarshipsConnects participants with wine-industry mentors and apprenticeships, and offers multi-year scholarships for Napa County residents studying viticulture and enology at local community colleges.

Programs and services

  • Adult Literacy ProgramEnglish, math, financial, and computer literacy classes, including workplace math and Excel for supervisors, scheduled around vineyard work for Napa County farmworkers and their families.Program website
  • El Futuro Educational ScholarshipMulti-year scholarship for Napa County residents pursuing an associate degree in viticulture and enology at Napa Valley College or Santa Rosa Junior College.Program website
  • Leadership Program (CULTIVAR and Women's Leadership Workshop)Leadership development for emerging vineyard leaders through the CULTIVAR and CULTIVAR360 academies and a women's leadership workshop, building communication, decision-making, and team skills.Program website
  • Management Program (Supervisor Development Series)Experiential training for vineyard supervisors and emerging managers on communication, accountability, problem-solving, and day-to-day management tools, delivered through a supervisor development series and field trips.Program website
  • Patrick Foley Fields of Opportunity (Summer Mentor and Apprentice Programs)Mentorship pathway pairing participants with wine-industry mentors through an eight-week summer program, an apprentice track with career workshops, and an alumni network supporting vineyard career growth.Program website
  • Safety ProgramHands-on safety training built for vineyard conditions, covering first aid and CPR, tractor and equipment safety, pesticide safety, night-harvest protocols, and harassment prevention for vineyard crews.Program website
  • Viticulture ProgramField days, workshops, and certification prep classes on pruning, canopy management, irrigation, climate, wildfire risk, and vineyard technology for vineyard workers, including the annual Napa County Pruning Contest.Program website
  • Workplace Training ProgramPractical workshops on communication, teamwork, collaboration, and employee engagement that help vineyard crews and supervisors build healthier, more positive workplaces.Program website
