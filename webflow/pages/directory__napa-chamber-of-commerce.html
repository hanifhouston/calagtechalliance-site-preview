Overview

Napa Chamber of Commerce connects local businesses through membership, education, advocacy, and economic development. Entrepreneurs and established companies can explore networking events, workshops, business-support referrals, and opportunities to increase their visibility in the community. The Chamber also provides connections among businesses and stakeholders around relocation, expansion, and local business issues. Agricultural businesses can use these routes to engage with Napa's wider economy. Benefits depend on membership level, and events or additional services may have separate fees.

What they do

  • Business networking and educationHosts networking events and workshops for Napa County businesses and refers members to business support, including counseling through the Small Business Development Center.
  • Member visibilityPromotes member businesses through directory listings, member communications, promotional opportunities, and introductions within the local business community.
  • Economic development and advocacyAdvocates on local business issues and connects companies and stakeholders around economic development, relocation, and expansion across Napa's economy, including agriculture.
