Messaging

Boilerplate and key messages.

Use the boilerplate as written; shape the key messages to your audience.

Boilerplate · 70 words

California AgTech Alliance is a statewide platform connecting agricultural innovation, workforce development, and entrepreneurship across California. Anchored by University of California, Agriculture and Natural Resources and funded through California Jobs First, the Alliance aligns programs, investment, and infrastructure so partners in nine regions can do more together.

Copy ↗
Key messages
  • Connected: one network across nine regions.
  • Practical: programs delivered by partners who know their region.
  • Open: stories, tools, and data that any partner can use.

Story guide

How to pitch and shape a story.

Start with a person and a problem, not a program. Say what changed, in their words where you can, and name the region. Keep it to 400 words, add one photo with a credit, and send it through the contact form with Story as the topic.
